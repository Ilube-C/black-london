# University College London (UCL)

*University College London, in Bloomsbury, where Ladipo Solanke studied law, Kwame Nkrumah registered to read philosophy and Jomo Kenyatta worked as a language informant.*

University College London is the site of the institution described under [University College London](../Organisations/ORG-0016.md). [Ladipo Solanke](../People/PER-0011.md) studied law there from 1923 to 1927 before qualifying as a barrister.[^1][^2] [Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md) writes that in 1945, after enrolling at Gray's Inn, "Later on I registered in University College as well in order to read philosophy". He began a thesis on logical positivism under Professor Ayer.[^3] In the 1930s [Jomo Kenyatta](../People/PER-0017.md) earned much of his income as a linguistic informant at University College and the School of Oriental Studies, working among others for Lilias Armstrong on Kikuyu phonology.[^4]
