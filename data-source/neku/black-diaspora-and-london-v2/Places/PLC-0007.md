# Gray's Inn

*The Inn of Court in Holborn where Henry Sylvester Williams prepared for the Bar from 1897 and Kwame Nkrumah enrolled in 1945.*

Gray's Inn is the site of the [Honourable Society of Gray's Inn](../Organisations/ORG-0004.md). [Henry Sylvester Williams](../People/PER-0003.md) was admitted in 1897 "to prepare for legal qualifications", and was called to the Bar in June 1902.[^1] Clarke writes that he "qualified and practised at Gray's Inn".[^2] [Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md) recalled that on arriving in London in 1945, "As soon as I arrived in London, therefore, I enrolled at Gray's Inn and arranged to attend lectures at the London School of Economics."[^3] The office of the [West African National Secretariat](../Organisations/ORG-0024.md), which Nkrumah ran, was nearby at [94 Gray's Inn Road](../Places/PLC-0056.md).[^4]
