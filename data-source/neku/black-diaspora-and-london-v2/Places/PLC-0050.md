# Willesden

*Willesden, in north-west London, where C. L. R. James lived after returning to Britain in 1953.*

After fifteen years in the United States, [C. L. R. James](../People/PER-0014.md) returned to London in 1953. English Heritage records that he then lived in Willesden.[^1] In July 1970, according to evidence later given at the Mangrove Nine trial, a small group met at the Willesden home of the activist Selma James to settle the details of the [Mangrove march](../Events/EVT-0024.md).[^2] James spent his last years in Brixton, at [165 Railton Road](../Places/PLC-0051.md), from 1981 until his death in 1989.[^1]

- **Street.** The sources consulted do not give a street address in Willesden for James, or say whether the 1970 meeting was held at the same house.
