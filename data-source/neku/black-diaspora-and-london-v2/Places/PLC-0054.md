# 60 Burghley Road

*The house at 60 Burghley Road, north London, where Kwame Nkrumah rented a room from June 1945 to November 1947; it carries an English Heritage blue plaque erected in 2005.*

On arriving in London in 1945, [Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md) found a room at 60 Burghley Road. He lived there from June 1945 until November 1947, when he returned to the Gold Coast.[^1][^2] English Heritage records that his landlady, Mrs Florence Manley, regarded him "as a member of my family".[^1] From here he worked on the [Manchester Congress](../Events/EVT-0004.md) and ran the [West African National Secretariat](../Organisations/ORG-0024.md) at [94 Gray's Inn Road](../Places/PLC-0056.md).[^2] Sherwood notes that Ako Adjei helped him find the room, and that a letter of 23 November 1945 gives it as his home address.[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **District.** English Heritage places the house in Kentish Town. Sherwood places it in Tufnell Park.[^1][^3]
