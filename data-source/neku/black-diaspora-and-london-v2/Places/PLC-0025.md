# Court of King's Bench

*The common-law court in which John Adams Wood's libel action against Thomas Pringle was heard on 27 February 1833, with Mary Prince as a witness.*

The Court of King's Bench heard *Wood v. Pringle* on 27 February 1833 (see [the trial](../Events/EVT-0011.md)).[^1] John Adams Wood, [Mary Prince](../People/PER-0008.md)'s former enslaver, sued Thomas Pringle for libel over the *History*. Prince was the first witness for the defence.[^1] Her testimony about her life under slavery was reported at length.[^1] The verdict was split. Wood was awarded £25 in damages but not his costs.[^1] Six days earlier, a different court, the Court of Common Pleas, had heard Pringle's own libel action against the publisher Thomas Cadell ([Pringle v. Cadell](../Events/EVT-0010.md)).[^1]
