# Central Criminal Court (Old Bailey)

*The Central Criminal Court in the City of London, where William Cuffay was tried in 1848 and the Mangrove Nine in 1971.*

The Central Criminal Court, known as the Old Bailey, appears in the atlas through two political trials more than a century apart. In the September 1848 session [William Cuffay](../People/PER-0009.md) was tried with William Lacey and Thomas Fay for "levying war" against the Queen. He was convicted and sentenced to transportation for life (see [the trial](../Events/EVT-0014.md)).[^1] The printed Proceedings record the evidence of the police informers whose testimony convicted him.[^1] In 1971 the trial of the Mangrove Nine, including [Altheia Jones-LeCointe](../People/PER-0024.md), began in Court 1 on 5 October and ended on 15 December (see [the trial](../Events/EVT-0025.md)).[^2][^3] Jones-LeCointe conducted her own defence.[^2]
