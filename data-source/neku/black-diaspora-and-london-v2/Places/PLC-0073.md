# Kilburn

*Kilburn, in north-west London, where Stuart Hall and Catherine Hall bought a house together.*

[Stuart Hall](../People/PER-0022.md)'s memoir mentions "the house we bought together in Kilburn". He shared it with his wife, the historian Catherine Hall, and her mother later had her own flat there.[^1] The memoir gives no street address. Hall's other London places in the atlas are linked to his work, at [Carlisle Street](../Places/PLC-0070.md), [Stockwell](../Places/PLC-0071.md) and [Rivington Place](../Places/PLC-0074.md).

## Evidence notes

- **Dates.** The sources consulted do not say when the Halls bought the house or how long they lived there.
- **Name.** An earlier version of this atlas gave the place a different name. The memoir names Kilburn, and the record follows it.[^1]
