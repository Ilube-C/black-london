# St Pancras Town Hall

*St Pancras Town Hall on Euston Road, venue of Claudia Jones's first Caribbean Carnival in 1959, of a Paul Robeson reception and of her memorial meeting in 1965.*

St Pancras Town Hall was a regular venue for [Claudia Jones](../People/PER-0018.md) and her allies. In 1957 [Paul Robeson](../People/PER-0019.md), still denied a passport, sang by transatlantic telephone link to a gathering there. Duberman puts the crowd at about a thousand.[^1] On 30 January 1959 the *West Indian Gazette* staged its first [Caribbean Carnival](../Events/EVT-0019.md) in the hall.[^2] That July the *Gazette* gave a reception there for Robeson, who was back in Britain.[^3] Later in 1959 the hall was used for a memorial meeting for Kelso Cochrane and a meeting against racial hatred addressed by Norman Manley.[^4] On 27 February 1965, after Jones's ashes were interred at [Highgate Cemetery](../Places/PLC-0061.md), a public memorial meeting for her was held at "St. Pancras Town Hall, Euston Road".[^5]
