# Islington Town Hall

*Islington Town Hall, venue of the first International Book Fair of Radical Black and Third World Books in April 1982.*

The first [International Book Fair of Radical Black and Third World Books](../Events/EVT-0022.md) was held at Islington Town Hall in April 1982.[^1] Bunce and Field describe it as a "week-long literary festival" whose success "led to it becoming an annual event thereafter".[^1] It was organised by New Beacon Books, [Bogle-L'Ouverture](../Organisations/ORG-0030.md) and Race Today. [Jessica Huntley](../People/PER-0023.md) was a joint director.[^1][^2] The Huntley papers hold material on the second fair, held by 1983.[^2]
