# 121 Railton Road Squat

*The flat above a launderette at 121 Railton Road, Brixton, squatted by Olive Morris and Liz Obi from 1972–3; it later housed Sabarr Bookshop.*

[Olive Morris](../People/PER-0025.md) and Liz Turnbull, later Liz Obi, occupied a flat above a launderette at 121 Railton Road, Brixton, and fought off attempts at illegal eviction.[^1] Libcom's history dates the occupation to 1972 and describes a police eviction attempt in January 1973. A photograph of Morris climbing onto the roof later became the cover of the 1979 *Squatters Handbook*.[^1] The Lambeth Archives catalogue dates the squat to 1973 and says that they resisted three illegal evictions. It adds that "121 Railton Road went on to host Sabarr Bookshop" and remained a squat until 1999.[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **Date.** Libcom gives 1972 for the occupation; the Lambeth Archives catalogue gives 1973.[^1][^2]
