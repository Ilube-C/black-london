# 15 Theobald's Road

*The house at 15 Theobald's Road, Holborn, where the composer Samuel Coleridge-Taylor was born on 15 August 1875.*

[Samuel Coleridge-Taylor](../People/PER-0010.md) was born on 15 August 1875 at 15 Theobald's Road, Red Lion Square, Holborn, according to his first biographer, W. C. Berwick Sayers.[^1] Sayers writes that his parents, the medical student Daniel Taylor and Alice Hare, had married secretly. They "settled with a working-class family of the name of Holman, at 15, Theobald's Road".[^1] The birth was registered on 27 September 1875, and Coleridge-Taylor told Sayers that he had been named after the poet Samuel Taylor Coleridge.[^1] He returned to central London as a student at the [Royal College of Music](../Places/PLC-0030.md) from 1890.[^2]
