# Notting Hill

*The west London district of Notting Hill, scene of the 1958 riots, the founding of the British Black Panther Movement in 1968 and the Mangrove campaign.*

Notting Hill appears in the atlas through several events. After the anti-Black riots of 1958, [Stuart Hall](../People/PER-0022.md) and the Universities and Left Review Club worked with local Black activists, churches and the local Labour Party to form tenants' defence associations.[^1] [Amy Ashwood Garvey](../People/PER-0004.md), at [1 Bassett Road](../Places/PLC-0011.md), founded the Association for the Advancement of Coloured People, with [Claudia Jones](../People/PER-0018.md) as its general secretary.[^2] In April 1968 Obi Egbuna founded the [Black Panther Movement](../Organisations/ORG-0031.md) in Notting Hill.[^3] Police raids on [the Mangrove](../Places/PLC-0080.md) in All Saints Road led to the march of 9 August 1970 ([the Mangrove march](../Events/EVT-0024.md)).[^4]

## Connected people

- **[Stuart Hall](../People/PER-0022.md)** — Stuart Hall took part in anti-racist work in Notting Hill after the riots of 1958, through the New Left's Universities and Left Review Club.[^5]
