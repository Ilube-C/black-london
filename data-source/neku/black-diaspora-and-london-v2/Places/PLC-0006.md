# Brixton

*The south London district at the centre of Black political organising from the late 1960s, visited by Nelson Mandela in 1996.*

Brixton, in Lambeth, appears in the atlas through several people and organisations. After 1968 the [Black Panther Movement](../Organisations/ORG-0031.md) moved its centre from Notting Hill to [Shakespeare Road](../Places/PLC-0079.md) in Brixton.[^1] In November 1969 the teenage [Olive Morris](../People/PER-0025.md) was beaten by police in Brixton after intervening in the arrest of a Nigerian diplomat ([the Gomwalk incident](../Events/EVT-0026.md)).[^2] The [Brixton Black Women's Group](../Organisations/ORG-0032.md) was founded there in the mid-1970s.[^3] Race Today, with [C. L. R. James](../People/PER-0014.md), worked from [Railton Road](../Places/PLC-0051.md).[^4] In a Commons tribute of 2013, the local MP spoke of "my constituents in Brixton who celebrated and still remember Mandela's visit in 1996" (see [Mandela's visit to Brixton](../Events/EVT-0007.md)).[^5]
