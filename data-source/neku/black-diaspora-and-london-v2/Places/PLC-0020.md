# 19 Charles Street

*The grocery shop at 19 Charles Street, Westminster, opened by Ignatius Sancho and his wife Ann in 1774, from which he wrote many of his letters.*

When gout forced [Ignatius Sancho](../People/PER-0007.md) to retire from the Montagu household, the Duke of Montagu helped him and his wife Ann set up a grocery. Carretta writes that it opened in early 1774 at 19 Charles Street, Westminster, beside the family's home at No. 20.[^1] As a male householder there, Sancho could vote. He voted in the Westminster elections of 1774 and 1780 ([the Westminster elections](../Events/EVT-0009.md)).[^1][^2] He died on 14 December 1780. His letters were published two years later as [*Letters of the Late Ignatius Sancho, an African*](../Works/WRK-0004.md).[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **Shop or house number.** Carretta puts the shop at No. 19 and the home at No. 20. J. T. Smith's recollection, reprinted by Carretta, puts the shop at No. 20.[^1]

## Connected people

- **[Ignatius Sancho](../People/PER-0007.md)** — Ignatius Sancho kept a grocer's shop at 19 Charles Street, Westminster, from early 1774 until his death in 1780.[^3]
