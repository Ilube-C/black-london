# 164 Queen's Road

*Dr Harold Moody's home and surgery at 164 Queens Road, Peckham, from 1922 until his death in 1947, where Una Marson lodged from 1932 to 1935.*

[Harold Moody](../People/PER-0012.md) lived and practised medicine at 164 Queens Road, Peckham, from 1922 until his death in 1947. English Heritage writes that "while here [he] founded the League of Coloured Peoples".[^1] His practice in Peckham had begun in 1913 in King's Road nearby.[^1] [Una Marson](../People/PER-0013.md) lodged with the Moodys after arriving from Jamaica in 1932. She stayed until spring 1935 and edited the League's journal [*The Keys*](../Works/WRK-0010.md) from the house.[^2] Jarrett-Macauley describes how the Moodys' lodgers were drawn into the new [League](../Organisations/ORG-0019.md).[^3]
