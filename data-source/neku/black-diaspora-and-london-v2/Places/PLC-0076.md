# Walter Rodney Bookshop

*The Walter Rodney Bookshop at 5a Chignell Place, West Ealing, opened by Bogle-L'Ouverture in 1975 and repeatedly attacked by racists between 1977 and 1983.*

In October 1975 [Bogle-L'Ouverture](../Organisations/ORG-0030.md)'s bookshop opened in a former dress shop at 5a Chignell Place, West Ealing.[^1] It was renamed the Walter Rodney Bookshop in 1980, the year of the historian's assassination.[^1] London Remembers calls it the second Black bookshop in the United Kingdom.[^2] Oates records repeated racist attacks on the shop from 1977, including racist slogans in October 1979. The National Action Party claimed an attack in January 1983.[^1] [Jessica Huntley](../People/PER-0023.md) and Eric Huntley responded by helping to form an alliance of bookshops, Book Shop Joint Action.[^3] Minutes of meetings held at the shop in 1986 survive in the Huntley papers.[^3]
