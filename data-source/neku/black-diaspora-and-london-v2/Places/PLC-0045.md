# Ethiopian Legation

*The Abyssinian (Ethiopian) Legation at Princes Gate, Kensington, where Una Marson worked from October 1935 and for Emperor Haile Selassie during his exile.*

In October 1935, as Italy invaded Abyssinia, [Una Marson](../People/PER-0013.md) began working at the Abyssinian Legation at Princes Gate, Kensington. Jarrett-Macauley dates her start to 2 October 1935.[^1] Her workload grew when the Emperor, Haile Selassie, arrived in London in 1936.[^1] Busby writes that she was "later private secretary to Emperor Haile Selassie of Ethiopia while he was in exile in Britain".[^2] At the same time [C. L. R. James](../People/PER-0014.md), [Amy Ashwood Garvey](../People/PER-0004.md) and others were campaigning for Ethiopia through the [International African Friends of Abyssinia](../Organisations/ORG-0008.md).[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **House number.** The sources consulted give the address only as Princes Gate.
