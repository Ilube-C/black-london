# Stockwell Secondary Modern School

*The secondary modern school in Stockwell, south London, where Stuart Hall worked as a supply teacher after moving to London.*

After leaving Oxford for London, [Stuart Hall](../People/PER-0022.md) taught English at a secondary modern school in Stockwell. Martin Jacques's biography records this, and that Hall later lectured in film and mass media studies at Chelsea College.[^1] In his memoir Hall describes the job as supply teaching at Stockwell Secondary Modern School. About a quarter of the boys were Black, and they came from around Brixton and the Oval.[^2] The experience coincided with the racial violence of 1958 in [Notting Hill](../Places/PLC-0072.md), which Jacques describes as the start of Hall's lasting concern with how Britain responded to colonial migration.[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **Date.** Jacques dates Hall's move to London to 1957, and Hall's memoir to 1958.[^1][^2]
