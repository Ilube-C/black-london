# St Margaret's Church, Westminster

*The parish church beside Westminster Abbey where Ignatius Sancho married Ann Osborne in 1758 and Olaudah Equiano was baptised in 1759.*

St Margaret's Church, Westminster, appears in the atlas through two eighteenth-century Black Londoners. Carretta's edition of Sancho's *Letters* records that [Ignatius Sancho](../People/PER-0007.md) married Ann Osborne "on 17 December 1758 in St. Margaret's Church, Westminster".[^1] Less than two months later, on 9 February 1759, the young [Olaudah Equiano](../People/PER-0006.md) was baptised there as Gustavus Vassa (see [Equiano's baptism](../Events/EVT-0008.md)).[^2] In his *Narrative* Equiano writes that the eldest Miss Guerin, one of the sisters who had cared for him in London, persuaded his master to allow it: "so I was baptized in St. Margaret's church, Westminster, in February 1759, by my present name."[^3] The two entries fall within weeks of each other. The sources consulted do not show that Sancho and Equiano met there or at any other time.
