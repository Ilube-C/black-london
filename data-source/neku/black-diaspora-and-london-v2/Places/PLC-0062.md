# Drury Lane theatre

*The Theatre Royal, Drury Lane, where Paul Robeson sang in the London production of Show Boat in 1928 and gave a celebrated matinee concert on 3 July 1928.*

In 1928 [Paul Robeson](../People/PER-0019.md) sang "Ol' Man River" in the London production of *Show Boat*.[^1] Duberman places the production at the Theatre Royal, Drury Lane. He also describes the celebrated matinee concert Robeson gave there on 3 July 1928.[^2] *Show Boat* began the period in which, as Adi and Sherwood put it, Robeson and his family made their home in London in the late 1920s and 1930s.[^1] Two years later he played Othello at the [Savoy Theatre](../Places/PLC-0063.md) ([Othello, 1930](../Events/EVT-0020.md)).[^1]
