# Montagu House, Whitehall

*The Duke of Montagu's residence in Whitehall, where Ignatius Sancho served the Montagu family as butler and valet.*

[Ignatius Sancho](../People/PER-0007.md) spent his working life in the service of the Montagu family. The memoir prefixed to his *Letters* records that after the second Duke of Montagu died in 1749, the Duchess took Sancho on as her butler.[^1] Carretta adds that he later became valet to the new Duke.[^2] The family's residence in the Privy Gardens, Whitehall, took the name Montagu House.[^2] When gout forced Sancho to retire from service in 1773, the Duke helped him set up the grocery at [19 Charles Street](../Places/PLC-0020.md).[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **Which house and when.** The sources consulted do not say which years Sancho spent at the Whitehall house, as distinct from the family's other residences.
