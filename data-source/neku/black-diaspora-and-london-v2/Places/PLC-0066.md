# Albert Hall

*The Royal Albert Hall in Kensington, where Paul Robeson gave a sold-out concert soon after his passport was restored in 1958.*

When [Paul Robeson](../People/PER-0019.md)'s passport was returned in 1958, he came back to Britain.[^1] Duberman records that soon after his return he gave a sold-out formal concert at the Albert Hall, with Larry Brown as his accompanist.[^2] Adi and Sherwood note that his British performances in these years also included St Paul's Cathedral, the Eisteddfod at Ebbw Vale and *Othello* at Stratford.[^1] A year earlier, still without a passport, he had sung to a London audience by telephone link at [St Pancras Town Hall](../Places/PLC-0059.md).[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **Date.** Duberman places the concert soon after Robeson's return in 1958. The sources consulted do not give the exact date.
