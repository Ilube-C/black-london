# 38 Church Street

*The house at 38 Church Street, Marylebone, which Westminster City Council describes as Henry Sylvester Williams's former home and marks with a green plaque.*

Westminster City Council commemorates [Henry Sylvester Williams](../People/PER-0003.md) with a green plaque at 38 Church Street, Marylebone, which it describes as his former home.[^1] The council's report calls him the first Black councillor in Westminster. In November 1906 he won a seat on [St Marylebone Borough Council](../Organisations/ORG-0006.md) for the Church Street ward.[^1] Fryer records that he stood as a "Progressive" and polled 701 votes.[^2] Sherwood counts him as "one of the first two elected Black borough councillors in London".[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Dates of residence.** The council's report does not say when Williams lived at the house, and the other sources consulted do not give the address.
