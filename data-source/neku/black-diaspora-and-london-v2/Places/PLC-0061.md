# Highgate Cemetery

*Highgate Cemetery in north London, where the ashes of Claudia Jones were interred on 27 February 1965, next to the grave of Karl Marx.*

After [Claudia Jones](../People/PER-0018.md) died in December 1964, A. Manchanda ("Manu") "bought a burial plot next to the grave of Karl Marx in Highgate cemetery".[^1] She was cremated at Golders Green on 9 January 1965.[^1] The programme for 27 February 1965 announced the "INTERMENT OF ASHES, HIGHGATE CEMETRY, SWAINS LANE", followed by a public meeting at [St Pancras Town Hall](../Places/PLC-0059.md).[^2] The grave was registered in the name of the [Committee of Afro-Asian Caribbean Organisations](../Organisations/ORG-0025.md).[^3] Sherwood describes later efforts, in 1982–4, by the Afro-Caribbean Organisation and others to raise money for a headstone.[^4]
