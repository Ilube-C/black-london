# South Africa House

*The South African embassy in Trafalgar Square, a focus of anti-apartheid protest, where Nelson Mandela later spoke.*

South Africa House, on Trafalgar Square, housed South Africa's diplomatic mission in London. Adi and Sherwood record that [Claudia Jones](../People/PER-0018.md) helped organise "the first five-day fast outside South Africa House to protest against apartheid".[^1] The Anti-Apartheid Legacy centre writes that the bomb used against the [ANC office](../Places/PLC-0068.md) in 1982 was assembled at the embassy.[^2] Welcoming [Nelson Mandela](../People/PER-0002.md) to Westminster Hall in July 1996, the Speaker told him: "Tomorrow, Mr President, you will walk across Trafalgar Square to South Africa House where once you were vilified".[^3] In a Commons tribute of 2013 the MP Nigel Evans recalled "the one occasion I heard Nelson Mandela speak in South Africa house".[^4]

## Evidence notes

- **Date.** Evans does not date the occasion, and the sources consulted do not establish it.

## Connected people
