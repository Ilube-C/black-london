# 141 Coldershaw Road

*The Huntleys' house at 141 Coldershaw Road, West Ealing, from which Bogle-L'Ouverture Publications worked from 1972 to 1975.*

Jonathan Oates writes that [Bogle-L'Ouverture Publications](../Organisations/ORG-0030.md) moved in 1972 into the front room of [Jessica](../People/PER-0023.md) and Eric Huntley's house at 141 Coldershaw Road, West Ealing. Sales to the public began there in 1973, mostly by mail order.[^1] Because the house had no permission for business use, the council issued an enforcement notice. A public inquiry was held at Ealing Town Hall in 1975.[^1] That October the bookshop moved to [5a Chignell Place](../Places/PLC-0076.md).[^1] Oates records that a plaque marking the firm's years at the house, 1972–75, was unveiled in October 2018.[^1]
