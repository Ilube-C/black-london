# Beckford Primary School

*Beckford Primary School in West Hampstead, where Beryl Gilroy became deputy head and then head teacher from 1968–9.*

[Beryl Gilroy](../People/PER-0021.md) returned to teaching with the Inner London Education Authority in 1968. Her *Guardian* obituary records that she became deputy head and then head of Beckford Primary School in West Hampstead.[^1] The Greater London Authority dates her headship to 1969 and calls her Camden's first Black headteacher.[^2] The obituary calls her London's first Black head teacher.[^1] Her direct work in schools ended in 1982.[^1] She published her autobiographical [*Black Teacher*](../Works/WRK-0023.md) in 1976.[^1] In 2022 the Greater London Authority announced an artwork celebrating her.[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **"First".** The Greater London Authority's claim concerns Camden, and the obituary's concerns London. The sources consulted do not test either claim.
