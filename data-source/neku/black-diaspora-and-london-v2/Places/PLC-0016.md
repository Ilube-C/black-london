# No. 10 Union Street

*No. 10 Union Street, near the Middlesex Hospital, the address from which Olaudah Equiano published and sold his Interesting Narrative in 1789.*

In 1789 [Olaudah Equiano](../People/PER-0006.md) published his autobiography himself. The title page of the first volume of [*The Interesting Narrative*](../Works/WRK-0003.md) states that it was "Printed for and sold by the author, no. 10, Union-Street, Middlesex Hospital", and also by booksellers including Mr Johnson of St Paul's Church-Yard and Mr Murray of Fleet Street.[^1] His dedication to the Lords and Commons is dated "Union-Street, Mary-le-bone, March 24, 1789".[^2] Walvin describes how Equiano raised the money in advance from subscribers.[^3] The address therefore marks where the book could be bought from its author in its first year. By 1796 he was living at [Plasterers' Hall](../Places/PLC-0017.md) in the City.[^4]
