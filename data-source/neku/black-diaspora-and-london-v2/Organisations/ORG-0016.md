# University College London (UCL)

*The London college where Ladipo Solanke studied law from 1923 to 1927, where Kwame Nkrumah registered to read philosophy, and where Jomo Kenyatta worked as a language informant.*

University College London appears in the atlas through three West and East Africans.

- **[Ladipo Solanke](../People/PER-0011.md)** studied law there from 1923 to 1927 and qualified as a barrister.[^1][^2]
- **[Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md)** writes that after enrolling at Gray's Inn in 1945, "Later on I registered in University College as well in order to read philosophy." He began a thesis on logical positivism under Professor Ayer.[^3]
- **[Jomo Kenyatta](../People/PER-0017.md)** earned his main income, after his return from Russia, as a linguistic informant at University College and SOAS. Among others, he worked for Lilias Armstrong on Kikuyu phonology.[^4] Murray-Brown records him on University College's books in 1935–36.[^5]

Its site is recorded under [University College London](../Places/PLC-0032.md).
