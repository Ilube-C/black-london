# Whitelands College

*The teacher-training college in Putney where Constance Cummings-John trained as a teacher in the mid-1930s.*

Whitelands College was a teacher-training college. Sierra Leone Web records that [Constance Cummings-John](../People/PER-0015.md) went to England at the age of seventeen "and entered Whitelands College in Putney, where she trained as a teacher".[^1] Adi and Sherwood write that she was sent to London in 1935 to train as a teacher and qualified in a year, though they do not name the college.[^2] While in London she took part in the [West African Students' Union](../Organisations/ORG-0018.md) and the [League of Coloured Peoples](../Organisations/ORG-0019.md).[^2] The college's location is recorded at [Whitelands College](../Places/PLC-0052.md).
