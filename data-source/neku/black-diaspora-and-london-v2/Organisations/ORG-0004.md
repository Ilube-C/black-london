# Honourable Society of Gray's Inn

*One of the Inns of Court in Holborn, where Henry Sylvester Williams prepared for the Bar from 1897 and Kwame Nkrumah enrolled in 1945.*

The Honourable Society of Gray's Inn admitted students preparing to be called to the Bar. Two figures in the atlas enrolled there.

[Henry Sylvester Williams](../People/PER-0003.md) was admitted in 1897, the year after he arrived in London, "to prepare for legal qualifications". He was called to the Bar in June 1902.[^1] Clarke writes that he "qualified and practised at Gray's Inn".[^2] [Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md) went to London in 1945 "to study law and, at the same time, to complete my thesis for a doctorate in philosophy". He writes: "As soon as I arrived in London, therefore, I enrolled at Gray's Inn and arranged to attend lectures at the London School of Economics."[^3] Its site is recorded under [Gray's Inn](../Places/PLC-0007.md).
