# Royal College of Music

*The London conservatoire where Samuel Coleridge-Taylor studied from 1890 to 1897, and where Hiawatha's Wedding Feast was first performed in 1898.*

The Royal College of Music trained [Samuel Coleridge-Taylor](../People/PER-0010.md). Sayers records that he "commenced his study there at the Christmas Term, 1890". He left "at the end of the Easter Term, 1897".[^1] In October 1892 the College's Director, George Grove, wrote to his patron: "I have arranged with Dr. Stanford to take Taylor with composition as his first study." From the Michaelmas Term of 1892, Sayers writes, Coleridge-Taylor studied under Charles Villiers Stanford, to whom he was deeply devoted.[^2] The College also gave the first performance of his cantata [*Hiawatha's Wedding Feast*](../Works/WRK-0007.md), at a concert conducted by Sir Charles Stanford on 11 November 1898 (see [the premiere](../Events/EVT-0015.md)).[^3] Its site is recorded under [the Royal College of Music](../Places/PLC-0030.md).
