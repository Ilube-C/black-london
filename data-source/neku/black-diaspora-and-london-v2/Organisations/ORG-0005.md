# King's College London

*The London college where Henry Sylvester Williams studied from 1896 and where, according to Fryer, Harold Moody studied medicine from 1904.*

King's College London appears in the atlas through two students from the Caribbean.

[Henry Sylvester Williams](../People/PER-0003.md) arrived in London in 1896 and, according to Adi and Sherwood, "enrolled at King's College as an evening student taking Latin".[^1] Clarke writes that he came "to pursue studies in Law at Kings College".[^2] [Harold Moody](../People/PER-0012.md) came to London from Jamaica in 1904. Fryer writes that he studied medicine at King's College and qualified in 1910.[^3] Adi and Sherwood describe him more generally as a medical student at London University from 1904, who took his degree in 1912.[^4] Its site is recorded under [King's College London](../Places/PLC-0008.md).

## Evidence notes

- **Williams's studies.** Adi and Sherwood record Latin in evening classes; Clarke records law.
- **Moody's qualification.** Fryer gives 1910; Adi and Sherwood give a degree in 1912 and do not name the college.

## Connected people

- **[Henry Sylvester Williams](../People/PER-0003.md)** — Henry Sylvester Williams studied at King's College London after arriving in the city in 1896.[^5]
