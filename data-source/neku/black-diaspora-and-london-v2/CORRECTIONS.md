# Corrections since the first edition


This edition corrects the following statements in the first edition of the atlas. Each correction rests on the source given.

- **Una Marson and Broadcasting House.** The first edition said that Marson worked at Broadcasting House in Portland Place. Her biographer states that "Una did not work there": her office was in the old Bedford College in Regent's Park, the temporary home of the BBC's Empire Talks Department.[^1] The biography of [Una Marson](People/PER-0013.md), the entry for the [BBC](Organisations/ORG-0020.md) and the entry for [Broadcasting House](Places/PLC-0046.md) now say so. Broadcasting House is kept as a record, so that the correction can be followed.
- **The imprint of Equiano's *Interesting Narrative*.** The first edition's source record gave a different street for the imprint of the first edition of 1789. The title page reads "Printed for and sold by the author, no. 10, Union-Street, Middlesex Hospital".[^2] See [No. 10 Union Street](Places/PLC-0016.md) and [the source record](Sources/SRC-0096.md).
- **Page references to the Westminster Hall speeches of 1996.** In the printed speeches, the Lord Chancellor's welcome is on p. 2, Nelson Mandela's address on pp. 3–7, the Speaker's reply on p. 8 and the order of proceedings on pp. 9–11. References that gave "pp. 1–3" or "pp. 7–8" have been corrected.[^3]
