# Visit to Brixton

*Nelson Mandela's visit to Brixton, south London, in 1996, remembered by residents and recalled in the Commons tributes of 2013.*

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Date | 1996 |

<!-- MEDIA: image slot — photograph of Mandela in Brixton, 1996; to be selected and added by the editor -->

In 1996, the year of his state visit to Britain, [Nelson Mandela](../People/PER-0002.md) visited [Brixton](../Places/PLC-0006.md) in south London. When the House of Commons paid tribute to Mandela in December 2013, the local MP spoke of "my constituents in Brixton who celebrated and still remember Mandela's visit in 1996".[^1] Brixton also appears in this atlas in the lives of [Olive Morris](../People/PER-0025.md) and [Altheia Jones-LeCointe](../People/PER-0024.md) (see [Brixton](../Places/PLC-0006.md)).

## Evidence notes

- **What is not recorded.** The sources consulted do not give the date of the visit within 1996, the route he took, the places he went to or anything he said. A contemporary newspaper report would be needed to describe it further.
- **Year and context.** The visit is recorded only in the 2013 tribute. Its connection with the state visit of July 1996 (see [Address in Westminster Hall](../Events/EVT-0006.md)) is assumed from the year and is not stated in that source.
