# *Sanders of the River*

*Alexander Korda's 1935 film of Edgar Wallace's colonial adventure stories, starring Paul Robeson as Bosambo, in which Jomo Kenyatta appeared as an extra.*

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Date | 1935 |

*Sanders of the River* is a film made by Alexander Korda's company from Edgar Wallace's stories of a British district commissioner in Africa. Early in the summer of 1934 the Korda brothers offered [Paul Robeson](../People/PER-0019.md) the role of the chief Bosambo.[^1] Korda needed some 250 Black extras. Murray-Brown records that [Jomo Kenyatta](../People/PER-0017.md) was one of them, playing a minor chief, for a guinea a day.[^2] The film opened at the Leicester Square Theatre on 2 April 1935.[^3] *The Times* wrote that it "will bring no discredit on Imperial authority".[^2][^4] Young African nationalists criticised it, and Kenyatta's part in it.[^2] Robeson later disowned the film, telling a reporter: "I hate the picture."[^5]

## Evidence notes

- **The premiere.** Marie Seton wrote that Robeson walked out of the opening night. Murray-Brown notes that others present do not corroborate this.[^3]
