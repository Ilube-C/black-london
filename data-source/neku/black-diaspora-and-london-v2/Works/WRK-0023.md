# *Black Teacher*

*Beryl Gilroy's memoir of her years as a teacher in London, first published in 1976.*

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Date | 1976 |
| Where to read it | [Beryl Gilroy, Black Teacher (London: Cassell (Faber reissue, 2021, with timeline, consulted), 1976)](../Sources/SRC-0053.md) |

*Black Teacher* is a memoir by [Beryl Gilroy](../People/PER-0021.md) of her years as a teacher in London in the 1950s and 1960s. It was first published in 1976.[^1][^2][^3] Her *Guardian* obituary records that the publisher softened the book for fear of its sales, but that it remained a harsher account of her working-class pupils, and of the obstacles facing Black teachers, than E. R. Braithwaite's *To Sir, With Love*.[^3] Racism at first kept her out of teaching, and Busby records that she worked as a shop clerk and a maid before she found a post.[^1][^4] [Bogle-L'Ouverture](../Organisations/ORG-0030.md) reprinted it in 1994, and Faber reissued it in 2021.[^3][^2]

## Evidence notes

- **Teaching dates.** The obituary dates her first teaching to 1953–56; the timeline in the 2021 edition gives 1954 for her first classroom post.[^3][^2]
- **Edition.** Busby's headnote gives 1976; her list of sources cites a Cassell edition of 1979.[^4][^5]
