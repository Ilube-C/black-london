# *Frangipani House*

*Beryl Gilroy's first novel, set in an old people's home in Guyana, published by Heinemann in 1986.*

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Date | 1986 |

*Frangipani House* is the first novel by [Beryl Gilroy](../People/PER-0021.md), published in 1986.[^1][^2] Busby describes it as set in "the twilight world of a Guyanese woman sent to an old people's home", where Mama King tries to come to terms with losing "her faithful friends, work and hardship".[^3] Her *Guardian* obituary says the novel explored issues of ageing, "hitherto absent in West Indian novels".[^1] Busby records that it won the Greater London Council's Black Literature Prize and prints an extract from chapter IV.[^2] Heinemann Educational Books published it in London.[^4]

## Evidence notes

- **The prize.** The timeline in the 2021 edition of *Black Teacher* dates the GLC Creative Writing Prize for *Frangipani House* to 1985, the year before publication. Busby names the award the GLC Black Literature Prize.[^5][^2]
