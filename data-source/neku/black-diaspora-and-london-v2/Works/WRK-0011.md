# *At What a Price*

*Una Marson's first play, a comedy first staged in Kingston in June 1932 and then by the League of Coloured Peoples in London in November 1933 and at the Scala Theatre in January 1934.*

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Date | 1932 |

*At What a Price* is the first play by [Una Marson](../People/PER-0013.md), a comedy about a young countrywoman who goes to work in Kingston as a stenographer and is seduced by her employer.[^1] It was staged at Kingston's Ward Theatre on 11 June 1932. Marson had devised it with her friend Horace Vaz, but Jarrett-Macauley calls it "chiefly her work".[^1] In London, Marson mounted a [League of Coloured Peoples](../Organisations/ORG-0019.md) performance at the YWCA's Central Club in Great Russell Street on 23 November 1933.[^2] It then transferred to the [Scala Theatre](../Places/PLC-0044.md) in Charlotte Street for three nights from 15 January 1934. Jarrett-Macauley calls it the first Black colonial production in the West End.[^2] The play was never published.[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Date.** Jarrett-Macauley dates the play to 1931 in one place and to 1932 in others.[^4][^3]
