# *New Left Review*

*The journal of the British New Left, formed in January 1960 from Universities and Left Review and The New Reasoner, with Stuart Hall as its first editor.*

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Date | 1960 |

*New Left Review* was formed in January 1960 when *Universities and Left Review* and *The New Reasoner* merged.[^1][^2] [Stuart Hall](../People/PER-0022.md) had been a founding editor of *Universities and Left Review* in 1957.[^3] He became the first editor of the merged journal, which he edited in 1960–61.[^1][^4] In his memoir he recalls that E. P. Thompson declined the post and that the New Reasoner group doubted Raphael Samuel could deliver copy on time: "So they asked me and I stupidly said 'Yes'."[^2] The New Left met in renovated offices in Carlisle Street, Soho, with a club, library and coffee bar (see [the Partisan Coffee House](../Places/PLC-0070.md)).[^5] Perry Anderson succeeded Hall as editor in 1962.[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **Merger or renaming.** The Bishopsgate Institute and Hall's memoir describe a merger of the two journals.[^1][^2] The *Oxford Dictionary of National Biography* says that *Universities and Left Review* was "soon to be renamed" *New Left Review*.[^3]

## Connected people

- **[Stuart Hall](../People/PER-0022.md)** — Stuart Hall was the first editor of New Left Review, in 1960–61.[^6]
