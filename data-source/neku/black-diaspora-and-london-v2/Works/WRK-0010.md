# *The Keys*

*The quarterly journal of the League of Coloured Peoples, published from 1933, which printed the League's aims in every issue; Una Marson edited it in its early years.*

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Date | 1933– |

*The Keys* was the journal of the [League of Coloured Peoples](../Organisations/ORG-0019.md), founded by [Harold Moody](../People/PER-0012.md) in 1931. Adi and Sherwood write that by 1933 the League was issuing it as a quarterly.[^1] Jarrett-Macauley dates its first appearance to the summer of 1933. She records that [Una Marson](../People/PER-0013.md), then lodging with the Moodys at [164 Queens Road](../Places/PLC-0039.md), became its editor.[^2] Busby likewise describes Marson as the League's secretary and the editor of its journal.[^3]

## Aims

Fryer writes that the League's four aims were "printed in each issue of its quarterly journal *The Keys*". They were: "To promote and protect the Social, Educational, Economic and Political Interests of its members"; "To interest members in the Welfare of Coloured Peoples in all parts of the World"; "To improve relations between the Races"; and "To co-operate and affiliate with organizations sympathetic to Coloured People".[^4]

## Contributors

Fryer records that the young St Lucian economist W. Arthur Lewis "flayed the pro-employers report of the commission of inquiry into the Trinidad disturbances" in the pages of *The Keys*.[^4]

## Evidence notes

- **Copies.** No issue of *The Keys* was consulted directly for this edition, and no open-access text was found. The account rests on Fryer, Adi and Sherwood, Jarrett-Macauley and Busby.
- **Run.** The sources consulted do not give the date of the last issue.
