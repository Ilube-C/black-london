# *Facing Mount Kenya*

*Jomo Kenyatta's study of Kikuyu life, based on his London School of Economics diploma thesis and published by Secker and Warburg in September 1938 with an introduction by Bronisław Malinowski.*

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Date | 1938 |
| Where to read it | [Jomo Kenyatta, Facing Mount Kenya (London: Secker & Warburg (Mercury Books reprint consulted), 1938)](../Sources/SRC-0046.md) |

*Facing Mount Kenya: The Tribal Life of the Gikuyu* is a study by [Jomo Kenyatta](../People/PER-0017.md). It grew out of papers he gave in Malinowski's seminars at the [London School of Economics](../Organisations/ORG-0023.md) and his diploma thesis, submitted at the end of 1937.[^1] Berman writes that Dinah Stock of the Independent Labour Party edited the thesis into a book in about three weeks. [C. L. R. James](../People/PER-0014.md) introduced Kenyatta to his own publisher, Frederic Warburg.[^1] Secker and Warburg published it in September 1938, a few weeks before James's [*The Black Jacobins*](../Works/WRK-0015.md).[^1][^2] In his introduction Malinowski called it "one of the first really competent and instructive contributions to African ethnography by a scholar of pure African parentage".[^3] James reviewed it in [*International African Opinion*](../Works/WRK-0013.md) in August 1938.[^4]
