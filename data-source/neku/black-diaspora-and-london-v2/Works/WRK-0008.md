*Twenty-Four Negro Melodies, Transcribed for the Piano*, Op. 59, is a collection by [Samuel Coleridge-Taylor](../People/PER-0010.md). Oliver Ditson published it in Boston in 1905, with a preface by Booker T. Washington.[^1] A digitised copy is available through HathiTrust.[^1] Coleridge-Taylor had taken charge of the music at the [Pan-African Conference](../Events/EVT-0001.md) in Westminster Town Hall in July 1900.[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **The collection's origins.** The sources consulted for this edition do not discuss how or why Coleridge-Taylor made the collection.
