# *A History of Negro Revolt*

*C. L. R. James's short survey of Black resistance and revolt, written for Raymond Postgate's Fact series and published in September 1938.*

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Date | 1938 |

*A History of Negro Revolt* is a short book by [C. L. R. James](../People/PER-0014.md). Høgsbjerg records that the socialist historian Raymond Postgate asked [George Padmore](../People/PER-0005.md) for a piece on "Negro revolt" for his monthly *Fact*. Padmore referred him to James, who wrote it rapidly and finished by April 1938.[^1] It was published in September 1938. James remembered that it "could be seen on all bookshops and railway stalls".[^2] Adi and Sherwood list it among James's London works of 1938.[^3] William Harrison, a fellow member of the [International African Service Bureau](../Organisations/ORG-0009.md), reviewed it critically in [*International African Opinion*](../Works/WRK-0013.md) in October 1938.[^4]
