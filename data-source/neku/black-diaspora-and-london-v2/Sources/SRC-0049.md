## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Paul Robeson](../People/PER-0019.md): Starred in Show Boat at Drury Lane in 1928. — p. 115: a matinee concert 'in the vast Drury Lane theater, where Show Boat performed evenings'; the Drury Lane concert 'on July 3'
- [Paul Robeson](../People/PER-0019.md): Opened as Othello at the Savoy Theatre, London, on 19 May 1930. — Ch. 'Othello (1930-1931)', pp. 133–7: rehearsals from early April and the opening night; the notes cite London reviews dated 20 May 1930. The theatre is not named
- [Paul Robeson](../People/PER-0019.md): Led the cast of C. L. R. James's play Toussaint Louverture in 1936. — p. 194: he accepted an offer 'to portray Toussaint L'Ouverture in a stage play by the radical Trinidadian C. L. R. James'; p. 197: 'Sponsored by the Stage Society, the play was given on several Sunday evenings in March 1936'
