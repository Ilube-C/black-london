## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Henry Sylvester Williams](../People/PER-0003.md): Elected to Marylebone Council in 1906. — ch. 9, 'Challenges to empire', 'The Pan-African Conference (1900) and after': 'In November 1906 Williams … won a seat on Marylebone borough council as a “Progressive” … polling 701 votes in the Church Street ward'
- [William Cuffay](../People/PER-0009.md): Westminster's representative on the National Charter Association's metropolitan district council from 1841, and its treasurer from 1842. — ch. 9, 'William Cuffay': 'in 1841 the Westminster Chartists sent him to represent them on the Metropolitan Delegate Council'
- [Samuel Coleridge-Taylor](../People/PER-0010.md): Elected to the executive committee of the Pan-African Association, into which the African Association was merged at the July 1900 conference. — ch. 9, 'Samuel Coleridge-Taylor': he 'was elected to the executive committee of the Pan-African Association'; 'The rise of Pan-Africanism': 'The African Association was merged into a new Pan-African Association'
- [Harold Moody](../People/PER-0012.md): Founded the League of Coloured Peoples in 1931. — ch. 10, 'Dr Harold Moody and the League of Coloured Peoples': 'born at a meeting in the Central YMCA, Tottenham Court Road, London, on 13 March 1931'
- [George Padmore](../People/PER-0005.md): George Padmore and Jomo Kenyatta worked together from 1930, in Padmore's Comintern-backed International Trade Union Committee of Negro Workers and later in London's IAFA and IASB. — ch. 10, subsection 'The Pan-Africanist radicals' (Kenyatta worked closely with them in founding the IASB)
