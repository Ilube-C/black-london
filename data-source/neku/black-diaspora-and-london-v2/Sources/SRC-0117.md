## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Altheia Jones-LeCointe](../People/PER-0024.md): Helped set up the action group behind the Mangrove demonstration of 9 August 1970, and was tried for riot at the march. — 'The following year nine men and women were put on trial at the Old Bailey for causing a riot at the march'; 'Both Darcus Howe and Althea Jones-Lecointe took the unusual step of defending themselves at the trial'
- [Altheia Jones-LeCointe](../People/PER-0024.md): Conducted her own defence at the 1971 Mangrove Nine trial at the Old Bailey; all nine defendants were acquitted of the most serious charges. — 'Both Darcus Howe and Althea Jones-Lecointe took the unusual step of defending themselves at the trial.'
