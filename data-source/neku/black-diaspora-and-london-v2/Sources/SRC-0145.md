## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Mary Prince](../People/PER-0008.md): Briefly gave evidence in Pringle v. Cadell, heard in the Court of Common Pleas on 21 February 1833, to confirm that she had dictated her account for Pringle; she was not cross-examined. — ms. p. 9: Pringle's libel 'was brought to the Court of Common Pleas'; ms. p. 12: 'the trial was heard on 21 February 1833'; Prince 'was briefly called as witness, but only to confirm that she had dictated her account for Pringle and she was not cross-examined'
- [Mary Prince](../People/PER-0008.md): Gave evidence, as the first defence witness, in Wood's libel action against Pringle, heard in the Court of King's Bench on 27 February 1833 and reported in The Times on 1 March 1833 (Lambert); Salih's notes date the case to March 1833. — ms. p. 14: Wood v. Pringle 'heard before another of England’s common-law courts, the Court of King’s Bench, on 27 February 1833'; ms. p. 15: 'The first witness called was Prince herself' (The Times, 1 March 1833)
