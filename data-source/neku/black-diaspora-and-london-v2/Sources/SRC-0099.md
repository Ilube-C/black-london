## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Altheia Jones-LeCointe](../People/PER-0024.md): At the 1971 Mangrove Nine trial she was acquitted of the most serious charges (riot) but convicted of causing an affray and of assaulting police officers; sentenced to fifteen months' imprisonment, suspended for two years. — 'The defendants were acquitted of 23 of the 32 charges, including the most serious ones of riot.'
- [Altheia Jones-LeCointe](../People/PER-0024.md): Conducted her own defence at the 1971 Mangrove Nine trial at the Old Bailey; all nine defendants were acquitted of the most serious charges. — 'on 5 October 1971, the trial began at The Old Bailey'; 'self-representation for Altheia Jones-LeCointe and Darcus Howe'
