## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md): Birth date recorded by the priest who baptised him as 21 September 1909, the date he used on official documents; he himself thought 18 September 1909 likely. — p. 1: the priest who baptised him 'recorded my birth date as 21st September, 1909'; p. 3: he concludes he was probably born on Saturday 18 September 1909
- [Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md): Resided at 60 Burghley Road. — p. 51: the room at No. 60 Burghley Road was his home 'from June 1945 until November 1947'
- [Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md): Enrolled at Gray's Inn and attended lectures at the LSE on arriving in London; later also registered at University College. — p. 51: 'I enrolled at Gray's Inn and arranged to attend lectures at the London School of Economics'; 'Later on I registered in University College as well'
- [Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md): Worked with George Padmore organising the 1945 Manchester Pan-African Congress. — p. 52: 'George Padmore and I became joint secretaries of the Organisation Committee'
- [Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md): One of the founders and general secretary of the West African National Secretariat, formed after the 1945 Congress. — p. 55: 'I was offered and accepted the secretaryship of the West African National Secretariat'; office 'in 94 Gray's Inn Road'
