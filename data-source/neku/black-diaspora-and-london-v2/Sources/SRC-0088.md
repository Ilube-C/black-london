## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [William Cuffay](../People/PER-0009.md): Arrested on the evening of 18 August 1848 at his lodging, 11 Hollins Street, Wardour Street, Soho, two days after the arrests at the Orange Tree (16 August). — Ref. t18480918-2055 (trial no. 2181), evidence of Joseph Thompson (police-sergeant, F 11): 'I went to 11, Hollins-street, Wardour-street, Soho, on the evening of 18th Aug. last … I there found Cuffey, and took him into custody'; another witness: 'evening of the 16th, I knew that the parties at the Orange Tree had been apprehended'
- [William Cuffay](../People/PER-0009.md): Tried at the Central Criminal Court in the September 1848 session with William Lacey and Thomas Fay for feloniously compassing to levy war against the Queen; found guilty and sentenced to transportation for life. — Ref. t18480918-2055 (trial no. 2181), indictment ('feloniously did compass, imagine, devise, and intend, to levy war against the Queen') and verdict ('CUFFEY— GUILTY. Aged 60.' … 'Transported for Life.')
