## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Stuart Hall](../People/PER-0022.md): Edited New Left Review in 1960–1961, after editing Universities & Left Review in 1957–1959. — 'The first editor-in-chief of the merged publication was Stuart Hall'; 'Hall was succeeded as editor in 1962 by Perry Anderson.'
- [Stuart Hall](../People/PER-0022.md): Co-founded the Partisan Coffee House at 7 Carlisle Street, Soho, in 1958 with Raphael Samuel and Eric Hobsbawm (Bishopsgate); Hall's own account credits the café to Samuel. Universities and Left Review had offices upstairs, where Hall edited the journal. — Administrative history: 'In 1958, Samuel, along with Stuart Hall and Eric Hobsbawm, founded the Partisan Coffee House as a radical venue of the New Left, at 7 Carlisle Street in the Soho district of London ... a library and offices of the ULR were upstairs.'
