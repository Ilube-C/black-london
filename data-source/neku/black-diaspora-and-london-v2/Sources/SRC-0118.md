## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Ignatius Sancho](../People/PER-0007.md): Served the Duchess of Montagu as butler until her death, then the Duke as his valet, until gout ended his service towards the close of 1773. — Memoir (long s modernised), p. viii: 'admit him into her household, where he remained as butler till her death'; p. x: 'the present Duke soon placed him about his person'; 'Towards the close of the year 1773, repeated attacks of the gout and a constitutional corpulence rendered him incapable of farther attendance in the Duke's family'
- [Ignatius Sancho](../People/PER-0007.md): Kept a grocer's shop with his wife after leaving the Duke's service towards the close of 1773. — Memoir (long s modernised), p. x: 'it enabled him and his wife to settle themselves in a shop of grocery'
