## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Olaudah Equiano](../People/PER-0006.md): Birthplace disputed between Essaka, Igboland (autobiography) and South Carolina (naval and baptismal records). — pp. 8–9: baptismal and naval records indicate he was born in America; p. 80: the register's 'born in Carolina'; pp. 147–8: the 1773 Racehorse muster gives 'So. Carolina' as his 'Place and Country where born'
- [Olaudah Equiano](../People/PER-0006.md): Baptised at St Margaret's, Westminster, on 9 February 1759. — p. 80: 'On 9 February 1759, in St. Margaret’s, Westminster ... “Gustavus Vassa a Black born in Carolina 12 years old” was baptized'
- [Olaudah Equiano](../People/PER-0006.md): One of the leaders of the Black abolitionists in London who styled themselves the Sons of Africa; led several of their delegations to the Houses of Parliament. — Equiano and nine other men signed 'The Address of Thanks of the Sons of Africa to the Honourable Granville Sharp, Esq.' on 15 December 1787 (index: 'Sons of Africa, 20, 257')
- [Olaudah Equiano](../People/PER-0006.md): Drew up his will on 28 May 1796 while living in Plasterers' Hall, Addle Street, in the City of London. — p. 365: 'Equiano was living in Plasterers’ Hall when he drew up his will on 28 May 1796'; the will mentions the lease of 'Plasterers Hall situate in Addle Street'
