## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Olive Morris](../People/PER-0025.md): Intervened in the police arrest of the Nigerian diplomat Clement Gomwalk in Brixton on 15 November 1969, and was beaten by the police. — Springer, 'Stepping into the struggle', p. 9: 'on 15 November 1969, at the age of seventeen'; the man was 'Clement Gomwalk, a diplomat with the Nigerian High Commission'; her caption: 'after the police had beaten me up'
- [Olive Morris](../People/PER-0025.md): Squatted a flat at 121 Railton Road with Liz Turnbull (Liz Obi) from 1972 (libcom) or 1973 (Lambeth Archives catalogue), resisting attempts at eviction. — Whitfield, p. 98: '121 Railton Road, a flat the she and Liz Obi were squatting'; appendices, p. 124 (Lambeth Archives catalogue, IV/279): with Liz Obi 'they opened the 121 Railton Road squat in 1973, resisting three illegal evictions'
- [Olive Morris](../People/PER-0025.md): One of the founder members of the Brixton Black Women's Group, formed in 1973 (The Heart of the Race) or 1974 (Lambeth Archives catalogue; Liz Obi). — appendices, p. 125 (Lambeth Archives catalogue): 'co-founder of the Brixton Black Women’s Group in 1974'; Obi, 'Remembering Olive': 'a founding member of the Brixton Black Women’s Group in 1974'
