## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Stuart Hall](../People/PER-0022.md): Took part, through the New Left's ULR Club, in anti-racist work in Notting Hill after his arrival in London, including helping to put together local black and white tenants' defence associations. — p. 255: 'I’d left Oxford for London in 1958'; p. 258: 'by way of the New Left networks I became active in anti-racist work in Notting Hill'; p. 259: the ULR Club worked with local black activists 'to put together local black and white tenants’ defence associations'
- [Stuart Hall](../People/PER-0022.md): Had a house in Kilburn, which he describes as 'the house we bought together'; Catherine Hall's mother later had her own flat there. — p. 269: 'When John died she moved into her own flat in the house we bought together in Kilburn'
- [Stuart Hall](../People/PER-0022.md): Co-founded the Partisan Coffee House at 7 Carlisle Street, Soho, in 1958 with Raphael Samuel and Eric Hobsbawm (Bishopsgate); Hall's own account credits the café to Samuel. Universities and Left Review had offices upstairs, where Hall edited the journal. — p. 256: in the afternoons 'I would head up to Soho to the ULR office to edit the journal'; Raphael Samuel 'had managed to persuade his seriously reluctant fellow editors to support' the café; 'The majority vote was against'; p. 257: 'He raised funds'
