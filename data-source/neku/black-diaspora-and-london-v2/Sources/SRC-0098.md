## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Altheia Jones-LeCointe](../People/PER-0024.md): Led the British Black Panther Movement. — The National Archives, 'A public statement': Altheia Jones-LeCointe as Panther leader
- [Altheia Jones-LeCointe](../People/PER-0024.md): Helped set up the action group behind the Mangrove demonstration of 9 August 1970, and was tried for riot at the march. — 'A public statement': the Action Group 'was set up by anti-racist activists, like the leader of Britain's Black Panthers Altheia Jones-LeCointe'; 'Persecution of the Mangrove': it called for the 'end of the persecution of the Mangrove Restaurant'
- [Altheia Jones-LeCointe](../People/PER-0024.md): Conducted her own defence at the 1971 Mangrove Nine trial at the Old Bailey; all nine defendants were acquitted of the most serious charges. — 'all nine defendants were acquitted of the most serious charges after a long 55-day trial'
