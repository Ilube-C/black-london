## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Jomo Kenyatta](../People/PER-0017.md): Worked at University College London as a linguistic informant, including for Lilias Armstrong's work on Kikuyu phonology. — p. 326: 'work as a linguistic informant at University College and SOAS, his primary source of income after his return from Russia'; p. 340 n. 1: names Lilias Armstrong of University College, London, for whose work on Kikuyu phonology Kenyatta later worked as an informant (the scan's right margin is cropped)
- [Jomo Kenyatta](../People/PER-0017.md): Attended the London School of Economics, working for a diploma in anthropology under Bronisław Malinowski. — p. 314: 'By the spring of 1935, he was Malinowski's personal student'; p. 326: 'In the fall, Kenyatta enrolled for the three-year post-graduate diploma in social anthropology'; p. 331: thesis 'submitted at the end of the fall term of 1937'
- [George Padmore](../People/PER-0005.md): George Padmore and Jomo Kenyatta worked together from 1930, in Padmore's Comintern-backed International Trade Union Committee of Negro Workers and later in London's IAFA and IASB. — p. 331 (Kenyatta's IASB colleagues, 'notably Padmore, James, and Makonnen', put him in touch with Dinah Stock)
- [C. L. R. James](../People/PER-0014.md): C. L. R. James and Jomo Kenyatta worked together in the IAFA (James chairman, Kenyatta honorary secretary); James introduced Kenyatta to his publisher and reviewed Facing Mount Kenya. — p. 331 ('James introduced Kenyatta to his publisher, Frederic Warburg')
