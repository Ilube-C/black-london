## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [W. E. B. Du Bois](../People/PER-0001.md): Born in 1868 and died in 1963. — Header: '(23 Feb. 1868–27 Aug. 1963)'
- [W. E. B. Du Bois](../People/PER-0001.md): Attended the 1900 First Pan-African Conference. — Holt: "He had attended the first conference … in London in 1900"
- [W. E. B. Du Bois](../People/PER-0001.md): Took the initiative to hold the 1921 Second Pan-African Congress, held in Paris, London and Brussels. — Lists the 'Pan-African congresses held in Paris in 1919; London, Brussels, and Paris in 1921'
- [W. E. B. Du Bois](../People/PER-0001.md): Holt says that Du Bois co-chaired the opening session; Padmore's proceedings put him alone in the chair on 16 October. — 'Du Bois cochaired the opening session of the conference with Garvey's first wife, Amy Ashwood Garvey'
- [Amy Ashwood Garvey](../People/PER-0004.md): Padmore records that she chaired the opening session of 15 October; Holt says that she co-chaired it with Du Bois. — 'Du Bois cochaired the opening session of the conference with Garvey's first wife, Amy Ashwood Garvey'
- [W. E. B. Du Bois](../People/PER-0001.md): Holt records Du Bois joining Amy Jacques Garvey and George Padmore in sponsoring the 1945 Congress. — 'In 1945, however, Du Bois joined Garvey's widow, Amy Jacques Garvey, and George Padmore to sponsor' the Manchester conference
- [W. E. B. Du Bois](../People/PER-0001.md): Died 27 August 1963. — (23 Feb. 1868–27 Aug. 1963)
