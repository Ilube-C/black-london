## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Una Marson](../People/PER-0013.md): Born 1905, died 1965. — p. 221: headnote heading 'Una Marson 1905-65'
- [Una Marson](../People/PER-0013.md): Origin Jamaica. — p. 221: 'Jamaican feminist, journalist, playwright'; 'Born in St Elizabeth'
- [Una Marson](../People/PER-0013.md): Secretary of the League of Coloured Peoples in London (Busby); its unpaid assistant secretary from autumn 1933 (Jarrett-Macauley). — p. 221: 'by 1932, when she left for England. She was secretary of the League of Coloured Peoples in London'; 'Returning home in 1936'
- [Una Marson](../People/PER-0013.md): Edited the League of Coloured Peoples' journal, The Keys. — p. 221: 'secretary of the League of Coloured Peoples in London, and edited their journal'

- [Una Marson](../People/PER-0013.md): Radio broadcaster with the BBC World Service during her second stay in England, from 1938. — p. 221: 'During a second stay in England, from 1938, she was a radio broadcaster with the BBC World Service'
- [Una Marson](../People/PER-0013.md): Started the BBC programme Caribbean Voices. — p. 221: 'starting the Caribbean Voices programme'
