# Altheia Jones-LeCointe: Mangrove Demo

*Altheia Jones-LeCointe helped set up the action group behind the Mangrove demonstration of 9 August 1970 and spoke to the marchers before they set off.*

[Altheia Jones-LeCointe](../People/PER-0024.md) took a leading part in the [Mangrove demonstration](../Events/EVT-0024.md) of 9 August 1970. The National Archives records that the Action Group behind it "was set up by anti-racist activists, like the leader of Britain's Black Panthers Altheia Jones-LeCointe".[^1] Bunce and Field write that the march was organised "in conjunction with the Black Panther Movement". They record that before it set off, "Next to speak was Althea Jones-Lecointe, the driving force behind the British Black Panthers."[^2] She was one of the nine later tried for riot at the march (see [the trial](../Connections/REL-0177.md)).[^3]
