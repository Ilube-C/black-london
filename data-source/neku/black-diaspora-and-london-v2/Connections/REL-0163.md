# Jessica Huntley: 141 Coldershaw Road

*Jessica Huntley's household and the business of Bogle-L'Ouverture Publications moved to 141 Coldershaw Road, West Ealing, in 1972.*

Jonathan Oates writes that "The 4 Huntleys and their business moved to the front room of 141 Coldershaw Road in West Ealing in 1972".[^1] [Jessica Huntley](../People/PER-0023.md) and her husband Eric ran [Bogle-L'Ouverture Publications](../Organisations/ORG-0030.md) from [the house](../Places/PLC-0075.md) until the bookshop opened at Chignell Place in October 1975. A plaque unveiled in October 2018 marks the firm's years there, 1972–75.[^1]
