# Nelson Mandela: Address in Westminster Hall

*Nelson Mandela, as President of South Africa, addressed both Houses of Parliament in Westminster Hall on 11 July 1996.*

On 11 July 1996 [Nelson Mandela](../People/PER-0002.md), President of South Africa, addressed the members of both Houses of Parliament assembled in [Westminster Hall](../Places/PLC-0004.md) (see [the address](../Events/EVT-0006.md)).[^1][^2] The Lord Chancellor welcomed him and the Speaker of the House of Commons thanked him.[^3] Parliament's own record notes that he "addressed both Houses of Parliament in Westminster Hall on 11 July 1996".[^4] The text of his speech is described under [Address in Westminster Hall](../Works/WRK-0002.md).

[^2]: UK Parliamentary Archives, [*Speeches from Nelson Mandela's visit to Parliament, 1996*](../Sources/SRC-0004.md) (UK Parliament, 1996-07-11).
[^3]: UK Parliament, [*The President of The Republic of South Africa Mr Nelson Mandela: Speeches in Westminster Hall, 11 July 1996*](../Sources/SRC-0005.md) (UK Parliament, 1996-07-11), p. 2 (speech of the Lord Chancellor) and p. 8 (speech of the Speaker).
[^4]: UK Parliament, [*Tributes to Nelson Mandela*](../Sources/SRC-0007.md) (UK Parliament, 2013-12).
