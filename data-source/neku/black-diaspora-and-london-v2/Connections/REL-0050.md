[Harold Moody](../People/PER-0012.md) lived and practised medicine at [164 Queen's Road](../Places/PLC-0039.md), Peckham, from 1922 until his death in 1947. English Heritage, whose blue plaque marks the house, writes that he moved there in 1922, "where he lived, worked and died", and that "while here [he] founded the League of Coloured Peoples".[^1] His practice in Peckham had begun in 1913 in King's Road nearby.[^1]
