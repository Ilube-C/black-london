# W. E. B. Du Bois: First Pan-African Conference

*W. E. B. Du Bois attended the First Pan-African Conference at Westminster Town Hall in July 1900.*

[W. E. B. Du Bois](../People/PER-0001.md) attended the [First Pan-African Conference](../Events/EVT-0001.md), held at [Westminster Town Hall](../Places/PLC-0001.md) from 23 to 25 July 1900.[^1] Fryer records that he came as a delegate and spoke on the second day.[^2] Adi and Sherwood add that he chaired the committee that drafted the conference's appeal, ["To the Nations of the World"](../Works/WRK-0001.md) (see [Du Bois and the address](../Connections/REL-0002.md)).[^3] Thomas Holt's biography also records his attendance.[^4]
