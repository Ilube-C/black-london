# W. E. B. Du Bois and Amy Ashwood Garvey

*W. E. B. Du Bois and Amy Ashwood Garvey both took a leading part in the opening of the Fifth Pan-African Congress in Manchester in October 1945; the sources disagree about whether they chaired a session together.*

[W. E. B. Du Bois](../People/PER-0001.md) and [Amy Ashwood Garvey](../People/PER-0004.md) were both at the [Fifth Pan-African Congress](../Events/EVT-0004.md) in Manchester in October 1945, and both chaired sessions. The sources disagree about the opening session:

- **Holt** writes that "Du Bois cochaired the opening session of the conference with Garvey's first wife, Amy Ashwood Garvey".[^1]
- **Padmore's report**, published in 1947, gives Ashwood Garvey alone as chair of the first session, on 15 October, and Du Bois as chair of the sessions of 16 October.[^2]
- **Adi and Sherwood** write that Du Bois chaired all the sessions except the first, which Ashwood Garvey chaired.[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **What is documented.** The sources consulted place both at the Congress. They do not describe a meeting or conversation between them.

## Notes

[^1]: Thomas C. Holt, [*W. E. B. Du Bois (African American National Biography, ed. Gates and Higginbotham)*](../Sources/SRC-0001.md) (Oxford UP / Harvard Hutchins Center, 2008).
[^2]: George Padmore, [*Colonial and Coloured Unity: History of the Pan-African Congress*](../Sources/SRC-0074.md) (London, 1947), pp. 27, 32 and 37.
[^3]: Hakim Adi; Marika Sherwood, [*Pan-African History: Political Figures from Africa and the Diaspora since 1787*](../Sources/SRC-0133.md) (London: Routledge, 2003), 'George Padmore', p. 154.
