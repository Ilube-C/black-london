# Kwame Nkrumah: 60 Burghley Road

*Kwame Nkrumah lived in a rented room at 60 Burghley Road from June 1945 until November 1947.*

[Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md) lived at [60 Burghley Road](../Places/PLC-0054.md) from June 1945 until he left for the Gold Coast in November 1947.[^1][^2] English Heritage records that his landlady, Mrs Florence Manley, regarded him "as a member of my family".[^1] Sherwood notes that Ako Adjei helped him find the room, and that a letter of 23 November 1945 gives it as his home address.[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **District.** English Heritage places the house in Kentish Town; Sherwood places it in Tufnell Park.[^1][^3]
- **First weeks.** Sherwood writes that Nkrumah first stayed briefly at the WASU hostel and then with Adjei at 25 Laurier Road, Tufnell Park.[^4]
