# Olive Morris: Brixton Black Women's Group

*Olive Morris was a founder member of the Brixton Black Women's Group, formed in 1973 or 1974.*

[Olive Morris](../People/PER-0025.md) was one of the founders of the [Brixton Black Women's Group](../Organisations/ORG-0032.md). *The Heart of the Race* names her as "one of the founder members" and says that "she helped form the Brixton Black Women's Group".[^1] She had previously been in the Black Panther movement.[^2] The Lambeth Archives catalogue of her papers calls her a co-founder of the group, and Liz Obi calls her a founding member.[^3][^4]

## Evidence notes

- **Founding year.** A founder member recalls in *The Heart of the Race*: "We formed the Black Women's Group in 1973." The Lambeth Archives catalogue and Liz Obi give 1974.[^5][^3][^4]
