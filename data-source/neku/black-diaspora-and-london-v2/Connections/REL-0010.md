# Nelson Mandela: Address in Westminster Hall

*Nelson Mandela delivered the Address in Westminster Hall to both Houses of Parliament on 11 July 1996.*

[Nelson Mandela](../People/PER-0002.md) was the author and speaker of the [Address in Westminster Hall](../Works/WRK-0002.md), delivered to both Houses of Parliament on 11 July 1996.[^1] In it he spoke of closing "a circle which is two hundred years old", dating Britain's first entry into his country as a colonising power to 1795.[^2] Parliament printed the speeches of the day, and the Parliamentary Archives hold the record of the visit.[^1][^3]
