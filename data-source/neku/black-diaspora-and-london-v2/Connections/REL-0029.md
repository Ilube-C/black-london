# Olaudah Equiano: Sons of Africa

*Olaudah Equiano was one of the leaders of the Sons of Africa, the Black abolitionists in London who signed letters and addresses under that name in the later 1780s.*

[Olaudah Equiano](../People/PER-0006.md) was one of the leaders of the [Sons of Africa](../Organisations/ORG-0010.md). Adi and Sherwood write that he "became one of the leaders of the Black abolitionists in London, who began to style themselves the Sons of Africa".[^1] He signed their letters as Gustavus Vassa, among them the address of thanks to Granville Sharp of 15 December 1787.[^2] Fryer calls him "one of the acknowledged political leaders of his fellow-blacks", but "not by any means the only leader".[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Delegations to Parliament.** Adi and Sherwood state that Equiano "led several delegations of the Sons of Africa to the Houses of Parliament". Fryer infers that some of the signatories went with him to Westminster.[^1][^3]
