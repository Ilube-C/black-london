# Oliver Tambo: Tambo family house, Muswell Hill

*Oliver Tambo lived, when in London, at the house in Muswell Hill that his wife Adelaide bought.*

[Oliver Tambo](../People/PER-0020.md)'s family reached London in September 1960.[^1] Callinicos writes that Adelaide Tambo later bought a house in Muswell Hill. She raised the deposit after some fifteen years of working double shifts in hospitals.[^2] He calls it "the house in which Tambo was living", although Tambo was seldom in London. He records visitors at the house and Tambo's presence there in 1985.[^2][^3] See [the Tambo family house](../Places/PLC-0067.md).

## Evidence notes

- **Date and address.** The sources consulted do not date the purchase or give a house number.
