# Harold Moody and Una Marson

*Una Marson lodged with Harold Moody's family at 164 Queen's Road from 1932 to 1935 and worked for his League of Coloured Peoples as assistant secretary and editor of The Keys.*

[Una Marson](../People/PER-0013.md) lodged with [Harold Moody](../People/PER-0012.md) and his family at [164 Queen's Road](../Places/PLC-0039.md), Peckham, from 1932 until spring 1935.[^1] Jarrett-Macauley describes how the Moodys' lodgers were drawn into Moody's new [League of Coloured Peoples](../Organisations/ORG-0019.md).[^2] In autumn 1933 Marson became the League's unpaid assistant secretary, and she edited its journal, [*The Keys*](../Works/WRK-0010.md).[^3][^4] Busby describes her as the League's secretary in London.[^5]

## Significance

For Marson, Jarrett-Macauley writes, "The importance of the League ... was as a gateway to wider circles". Through it she found her first London audiences, including the League's production of her play [*At What a Price*](../Works/WRK-0011.md).[^6]
