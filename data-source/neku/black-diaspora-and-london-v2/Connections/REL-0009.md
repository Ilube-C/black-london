# Nelson Mandela: Westminster Hall

*Nelson Mandela spoke in Westminster Hall on 11 July 1996, when he addressed both Houses of Parliament.*

[Nelson Mandela](../People/PER-0002.md) spoke in [Westminster Hall](../Places/PLC-0004.md) on 11 July 1996, when he addressed both Houses of Parliament as President of South Africa (see [the address](../Events/EVT-0006.md)).[^1][^2] The order of proceedings records the ceremony in the hall: the Lord Chancellor and the Speaker conducted him in, and he spoke after the Lord Chancellor's welcome.[^3]
