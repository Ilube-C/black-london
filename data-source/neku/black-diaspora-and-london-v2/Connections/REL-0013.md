# Henry Sylvester Williams: King's College London

*Henry Sylvester Williams studied at King's College London after arriving in the city in 1896.*

[Henry Sylvester Williams](../People/PER-0003.md) arrived in London in 1896 and studied at [King's College London](../Organisations/ORG-0005.md). Adi and Sherwood write that he "enrolled at King's College as an evening student taking Latin".[^1] Clarke writes that he came "to pursue studies in Law at Kings College".[^2] The following year he was admitted to [Gray's Inn](../Organisations/ORG-0004.md) (see [Williams and Gray's Inn](../Connections/REL-0014.md)).[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **Subject of study.** Adi and Sherwood say Latin, as an evening student; Clarke says law.[^1][^2]
