# Amy Ashwood Garvey: Florence Mills Social Parlour

*Amy Ashwood Garvey ran the Florence Mills Social Parlour, a nightclub and restaurant in Carnaby Street, from 1936.*

[Amy Ashwood Garvey](../People/PER-0004.md) opened the [Florence Mills Social Parlour](../Places/PLC-0010.md), a nightclub and restaurant in Carnaby Street, in 1936.[^1][^2] Høgsbjerg writes that it "quickly became 'a haunt of black intellectuals'".[^1] Adi and Sherwood name [George Padmore](../People/PER-0005.md), [C. L. R. James](../People/PER-0014.md) and Ras Makonnen among those who went there.[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Partner.** Høgsbjerg names the clarinettist Rudolph Dunbar as her partner in the venture; Adi and Sherwood name Sam Manning.[^1][^3]
- **Address.** The street number 50 comes from the *Women Who Meant Business* profile.[^2]
