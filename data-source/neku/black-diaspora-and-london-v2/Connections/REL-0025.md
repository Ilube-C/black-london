# George Padmore: 22 Cranleigh Street (Cranleigh House)

*George Padmore lived at 22 Cranleigh Street, Somers Town, from 1941 to 1957.*

[George Padmore](../People/PER-0005.md) lived at [22 Cranleigh Street](../Places/PLC-0012.md) in Somers Town, near Euston, between 1941 and 1957.[^1] He shared the flat with his partner Dorothy Pizer.[^2] [Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md) recalled that when they prepared the [Fifth Pan-African Congress](../Events/EVT-0004.md) in 1945, he and Padmore "worked night and day in George's flat".[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Nkrumah's account.** Nkrumah does not give the address of the flat. The year falls within the period of Padmore's residence at Cranleigh Street given by the *Camden New Journal*.[^1][^3]
