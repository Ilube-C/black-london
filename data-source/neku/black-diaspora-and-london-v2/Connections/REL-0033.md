# Mary Prince: Leigh Street, Bloomsbury

*Mary Prince stayed with her enslavers, John and Margaret Wood, at their lodgings in Leigh Street, Bloomsbury, after arriving in London in 1828.*

[Mary Prince](../People/PER-0008.md) came to London in 1828 with her enslavers, John and Margaret Wood. In her *History* she describes their lodgings "when we came up to live in Leigh Street".[^1] The editor's notes place the street in Bloomsbury.[^2] There Mrs Wood set her to wash heavy loads of clothes although she was ill, and the Woods finally "opened the door and bade me get out".[^1] She left the house, and in November 1828 went to the [Anti-Slavery Society](../Organisations/ORG-0013.md) for help.[^1][^3]
