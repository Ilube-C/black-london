# Nelson Mandela: Nelson Mandela's Clandestine Visit

*Nelson Mandela spent about ten days in London in June 1962, his first visit to the city, weeks before his arrest in South Africa.*

[Nelson Mandela](../People/PER-0002.md) made his first visit to London in June 1962, during the months he spent secretly outside South Africa (see [Mandela's visit of 1962](../Events/EVT-0005.md)).[^1][^2] In *Long Walk to Freedom* he describes "my ten days" in the city. He saw [Oliver Tambo](../People/PER-0020.md), met British political leaders and did some sightseeing.[^3] He returned home in July and was arrested at a police roadblock.[^2]
