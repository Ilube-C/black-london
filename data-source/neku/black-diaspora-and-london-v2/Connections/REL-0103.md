# Kwame Nkrumah and George Padmore

*Kwame Nkrumah and George Padmore were joint secretaries of the organising committee of the Fifth Pan-African Congress in 1945, and worked together afterwards.*

[Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md) arrived in London in 1945 and joined [George Padmore](../People/PER-0005.md) in preparing the [Fifth Pan-African Congress](../Events/EVT-0004.md). He writes: "George Padmore and I became joint secretaries of the Organisation Committee and we worked night and day in George's flat."[^1] Adi and Sherwood record that Padmore "enlisted the recently arrived Nkrumah's help".[^2] At the Congress in Manchester both served as rapporteurs, Nkrumah on "Imperialism in North and West Africa" and Padmore on the Caribbean.[^3]

## Significance

Adi and Sherwood write that Nkrumah had been "recommended to Padmore from the USA by C.L.R. James", and that the work on the Congress began "a partnership which was to last until Padmore's death". After Nkrumah returned to the Gold Coast in 1947, Padmore kept in close touch from London. In 1957 he went to Ghana as Nkrumah's Advisor on African Affairs.[^4]
