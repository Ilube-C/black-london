# Olaudah Equiano: Baptism of Gustavus Vassa

*Olaudah Equiano was baptised as Gustavus Vassa at St Margaret's Church, Westminster, on 9 February 1759.*

[Olaudah Equiano](../People/PER-0006.md) was baptised at [St Margaret's Church, Westminster](../Places/PLC-0015.md), on 9 February 1759 (see [the baptism](../Events/EVT-0008.md)). The parish register, as transcribed by Carretta, records "Gustavus Vassa a Black born in Carolina 12 years old".[^1] In his *Narrative* Equiano writes that he was "baptized in St. Margaret's church, Westminster, in February 1759, by my present name", with Miss Guerin as his godmother.[^2] Walvin gives the same month and church.[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Birthplace.** The register's "born in Carolina" is one of the records in the dispute over Equiano's birthplace. Carretta questions the African birth described in the *Narrative*; Lovejoy defends it.[^1][^4]
