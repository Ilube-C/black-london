# Paul Robeson: The Chestnuts, Branch Hill

*Paul Robeson lived at The Chestnuts, 1 Branch Hill, Hampstead, in 1929–30.*

English Heritage records that "At the peak of his fame in 1929–30" [Paul Robeson](../People/PER-0019.md) lived at [The Chestnuts](../Places/PLC-0064.md), 1 Branch Hill, Hampstead. Its blue plaque gives the dates 1929–1930.[^1] These were the months between his success in *Show Boat* at [Drury Lane](../Places/PLC-0062.md) in 1928 and his first *Othello* at the [Savoy Theatre](../Places/PLC-0063.md) in 1930.[^2]
