# Ignatius Sancho: Westminster Parliamentary Elections

*Ignatius Sancho voted in the Westminster parliamentary elections of 1774 and 1780.*

| | |

[Ignatius Sancho](../People/PER-0007.md) voted in the [parliamentary elections for Westminster](../Events/EVT-0009.md) in 1774 and 1780.[^1][^2] Carretta explains that his "financial independence as a male householder in Westminster" qualified him to vote.[^1] The Eighteenth-Century Political Participation and Electoral Culture project likewise records him as a voter in both elections.[^2]

## Notes

[^1]: Ignatius Sancho (ed. Vincent Carretta), [*Letters of the Late Ignatius Sancho, an African*](../Sources/SRC-0082.md) (Penguin Classics, 1998), introduction, p. xiv.
[^2]: Gillian Williamson (ECPPEC), [*Black Voters in the Archive*](../Sources/SRC-0140.md) (Eighteenth-Century Political Participation and Electoral Culture (ECPPEC), Newcastle University).
