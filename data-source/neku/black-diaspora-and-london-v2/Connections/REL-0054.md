# Una Marson: British Broadcasting Corporation (BBC)

*Una Marson worked for the BBC during her second stay in England, as a full-time programme assistant on the Empire Service from March 1941 until 1946.*

[Una Marson](../People/PER-0013.md) worked for the [BBC](../Organisations/ORG-0020.md) during her second stay in England. Jarrett-Macauley records that on 3 March 1941, "her BBC trial period over", she was "appointed full-time programme assistant on the Empire Service".[^1] She devised the programme [*Caribbean Voices*](../Works/WRK-0012.md), first broadcast in March 1943.[^2] She returned to Jamaica in April 1946.[^3] Busby likewise describes her as a radio broadcaster with the BBC.[^4]

## Evidence notes

- **Where she worked.** Her biographer states that Marson did not work at Broadcasting House; her office was in the old Bedford College in Regent's Park.[^5]
