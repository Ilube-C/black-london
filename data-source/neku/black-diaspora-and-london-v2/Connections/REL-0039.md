# Samuel Coleridge-Taylor: First Pan-African Conference

*Samuel Coleridge-Taylor took charge of the music at the First Pan-African Conference in July 1900 and was elected to the executive of the new Pan-African Association.*

[Samuel Coleridge-Taylor](../People/PER-0010.md) took part in the [First Pan-African Conference](../Events/EVT-0001.md) at [Westminster Town Hall](../Places/PLC-0001.md) in July 1900. Fryer writes that he "took charge of the musical side of the programme" and was elected to the executive committee of the Pan-African Association formed there.[^1] Clarke likewise records that he "provided the musical offering and later joined the committee".[^2] Richards calls him the youngest delegate.[^3]
