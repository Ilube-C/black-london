# Beryl Gilroy: Beckford Primary School

*Beryl Gilroy became deputy head and then head of Beckford Primary School in West Hampstead, from 1968–69.*

[Beryl Gilroy](../People/PER-0021.md) returned to teaching with the Inner London Education Authority in 1968. Her *Guardian* obituary records that "In 1968, she became deputy head and then head of Beckford primary school".[^1] The Greater London Authority dates her headship to 1969 and calls her "Camden's first black Headteacher".[^2] The obituary calls her London's first Black head teacher.[^1] See [Beckford Primary School](../Places/PLC-0069.md).

## Evidence notes

- **Dates.** The timeline in the 2021 edition of *Black Teacher* gives 1968–9 as deputy headteacher at Montem Primary School and 1969–82 as headteacher at Beckford.[^3]
