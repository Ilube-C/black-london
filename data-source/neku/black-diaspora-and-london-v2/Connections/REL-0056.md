# C. L. R. James: International African Friends of Abyssinia

*C. L. R. James founded and chaired the International African Friends of Abyssinia, formed in London in 1935 against Italy's war on Ethiopia.*

[C. L. R. James](../People/PER-0014.md) founded and chaired the [International African Friends of Abyssinia](../Organisations/ORG-0008.md) (IAFA). Høgsbjerg describes how James and [Amy Ashwood Garvey](../People/PER-0004.md) revived an earlier committee in 1935, which became the IAFA, and "James became chair".[^1] Høgsbjerg dates his chairmanship to 1935–36.[^2] Adi and Sherwood write that "Under the leadership of James" the group was formed in 1935.[^3] The IAFA campaigned against the Italian invasion of Ethiopia.[^4]
