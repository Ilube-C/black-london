# Kwame Nkrumah: West African National Secretariat (WANS)

*Kwame Nkrumah was a founder and the general secretary of the West African National Secretariat, formed in London after the 1945 Congress.*

[Kwame Nkrumah](../People/PER-0016.md) was a founder and the general secretary of the [West African National Secretariat](../Organisations/ORG-0024.md) (WANS), formed in London at the end of 1945.[^1][^2] He recalled that he "was offered and accepted the secretaryship" and that its office was at [94 Gray's Inn Road](../Places/PLC-0056.md).[^3] A fellow member remembered him as "the moving spirit from the beginning".[^4] He also edited its paper, [*The New African*](../Works/WRK-0018.md).[^5]
