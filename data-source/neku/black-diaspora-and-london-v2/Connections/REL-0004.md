# W. E. B. Du Bois: First Universal Races Congress

*W. E. B. Du Bois was an honorary secretary of the First Universal Races Congress in London in July 1911 and contributed a paper to it.*

[W. E. B. Du Bois](../People/PER-0001.md) was the honorary secretary for the United States of the [First Universal Races Congress](../Events/EVT-0002.md), which met at the University of London from 26 to 29 July 1911.[^1] The published papers of the Congress include his "The Negro Race in the United States of America".[^1] Adi and Sherwood write that he returned to London for the Congress and presented the paper there.[^2]
