# Henry Sylvester Williams: 38 Church Street

*Henry Sylvester Williams is commemorated by a Westminster City Council green plaque at 38 Church Street, Marylebone, his former home.*

Westminster City Council commemorates [Henry Sylvester Williams](../People/PER-0003.md) with a green plaque at [38 Church Street](../Places/PLC-0009.md), Marylebone, which it describes as his former home.[^1] The council's report calls him the first Black councillor in Westminster. He had been elected for the Church Street ward of [St Marylebone Borough Council](../Organisations/ORG-0006.md) in 1906.[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **The house.** The council describes the address as his former home. The sources consulted do not give the years he lived there.
