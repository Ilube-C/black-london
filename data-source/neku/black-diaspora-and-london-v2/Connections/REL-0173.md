# Altheia Jones-LeCointe: British Black Panther Movement

*Altheia Jones-LeCointe led the British Black Panther Movement from the end of 1968.*

[Altheia Jones-LeCointe](../People/PER-0024.md) led the [Black Panther Movement](../Organisations/ORG-0031.md) in Britain. Wild writes that after the founder Obi Egbuna withdrew at the end of 1968, "Althea Lecointe, a Trinidadian postgraduate student, became the Panthers' new leader".[^1] Under her leadership the movement's centre moved to [Brixton](../Places/PLC-0006.md).[^2] The National Archives calls her "the leader of Britain's Black Panthers".[^3] Bunce and Field call her "the driving force behind the British Black Panthers".[^4]

## Evidence notes

- **Spelling.** The sources spell her name in several ways, among them "Althea Lecointe" and "Althea Jones-Lecointe".[^1][^4]
