# Stuart Hall and C. L. R. James

*Stuart Hall held a conversation with C. L. R. James that was later published, and wrote a portrait of him.*

[Stuart Hall](../People/PER-0022.md) held a conversation with [C. L. R. James](../People/PER-0014.md) that was published as "A Conversation with C. L. R. James" in *Rethinking C. L. R. James* (1996). Hall also wrote "C.L.R. James: A Portrait" (1992).[^1] Høgsbjerg draws on the conversation for James's own account of his life. When James arrived in Britain in 1932, "as he later told Stuart Hall", he was "not at all" a Marxist.[^2] In his memoir, Hall quotes James's description of Caribbean migrants as "in, but not of, Europe".[^3]
