# Nelson Mandela: Brixton

*Nelson Mandela visited Brixton in 1996.*

[Nelson Mandela](../People/PER-0002.md) visited [Brixton](../Places/PLC-0006.md) in 1996 (see [the visit](../Events/EVT-0007.md)). The visit is recorded in the House of Commons tributes of December 2013, when the local MP spoke of "my constituents in Brixton who celebrated and still remember Mandela's visit in 1996".[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **What is not recorded.** The source consulted does not give the date, route or purpose of the visit.
