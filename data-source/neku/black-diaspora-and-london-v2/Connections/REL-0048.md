# C. L. R. James: 165 Railton Road

*C. L. R. James lived at 165 Railton Road, Brixton, above the offices of Race Today, in the last years of his life; he died there in 1989.*

[C. L. R. James](../People/PER-0014.md) spent his last years at [165 Railton Road](../Places/PLC-0051.md), Brixton. English Heritage writes that he lived there "from 1981 until his death eight years later", in a "cramped second-floor flat" above the offices of *Race Today*.[^1] Darcus Howe recalled in 1992 that James "lived with us, the small organisation, for the last ten years of he life ... He lived upstairs above the offices."[^2] The blue plaque reads: "C.L.R. JAMES 1901-1989 West Indian Writer and Political Activist lived and died here".[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **Dates.** Bunce and Field date the move of the *Race Today* offices to 165 Railton Road to mid-1982. The sources consulted do not resolve whether James lived there before the offices moved.[^1][^2]
