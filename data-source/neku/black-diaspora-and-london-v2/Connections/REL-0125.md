# Claudia Jones: West Indian Gazette Office

*Claudia Jones's newspaper, the West Indian Gazette, had its offices at 250 Brixton Road for most of its life.*

[Claudia Jones](../People/PER-0018.md) founded and edited the [*West Indian Gazette*](../Works/WRK-0022.md) (see [Jones and the Gazette](../Connections/REL-0126.md)). Donald Hinds, who worked on the paper, writes that it "spent the first five of its seven years of its existence in two crowded, chaotic and untidy rooms above Theo Campbell's record shop at 250 Brixton Road".[^1] English Heritage likewise places its offices at [250 Brixton Road](../Places/PLC-0058.md).[^2] Jones took legal advice from a solicitor "who had chambers above the office of the Gazette at 250 Brixton Road".[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Her presence.** The link is through the paper. The sources describe the office and its work, but none consulted separately describes Jones's own working days there.
