# Amy Ashwood Garvey: Nigerian Progress Union

*Amy Ashwood Garvey encouraged the founding of the Nigerian Progress Union in London in July 1924.*

The Nigerian Progress Union was founded by thirteen Nigerian students in London in July 1924. Hakim Adi writes that it was formed "largely due to [Amy Ashwood Garvey](../People/PER-0004.md)'s enthusiasm".[^1] Rhoda Reddock likewise records that she helped organise it.[^2] Its leading figure was [Ladipo Solanke](../People/PER-0011.md) (see [Solanke and Ashwood Garvey](../Connections/REL-0042.md)).[^1] The Union is described under [Nigerian Progress Union](../Organisations/ORG-0007.md).
