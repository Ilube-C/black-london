# Ignatius Sancho: 19 Charles Street

*Ignatius Sancho kept a grocer's shop at 19 Charles Street, Westminster, from early 1774 until his death in 1780.*

After leaving the Montagus' service, [Ignatius Sancho](../People/PER-0007.md) and his wife Ann set up a grocery with the Duke's help. Carretta writes that it opened in early 1774 at [19 Charles Street](../Places/PLC-0020.md), Westminster, "next to their new residence at 20 Charles Street".[^1] The memoir prefixed to his *Letters* records that he and his wife were enabled "to settle themselves in a shop of grocery".[^2] He kept the shop until his death on 14 December 1780.[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **Shop or house number.** Carretta puts the shop at No. 19 and the home at No. 20. J. T. Smith's recollection, reprinted by Carretta, puts the shop at No. 20.[^1]
