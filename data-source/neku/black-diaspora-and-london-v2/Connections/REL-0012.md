# Nelson Mandela: South Africa House

*Nelson Mandela spoke at South Africa House on Trafalgar Square, according to an MP's recollection; the date is not recorded.*

In the House of Commons tributes of December 2013, the MP Nigel Evans recalled "the one occasion I heard Nelson Mandela speak in South Africa house".[^1] This is the evidence that [Nelson Mandela](../People/PER-0002.md) spoke at [South Africa House](../Places/PLC-0005.md), South Africa's diplomatic mission on Trafalgar Square.

## Evidence notes

- **Date.** Evans does not say when he heard Mandela speak. Welcoming Mandela to Westminster Hall on 11 July 1996, the Speaker said that he would walk across Trafalgar Square to South Africa House the next day.[^2] The sources consulted do not show whether Evans was recalling that occasion.
