# Constance Cummings-John: Whitelands College

*Constance Cummings-John trained as a teacher at Whitelands College, Putney, after coming to England in 1935.*

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Connects | [Constance Cummings-John](../People/PER-0015.md) and [Whitelands College](../Organisations/ORG-0021.md) |
| Nature of the link | Constance Cummings-John was a member or student of Whitelands College. |
| Date | 1935–1936 |
| How it is documented | Documented link between a person and an organisation. |

Sierra Leone Web records that [Constance Cummings-John](../People/PER-0015.md) went to England at the age of seventeen "and entered Whitelands College in Putney, where she trained as a teacher".[^1] Adi and Sherwood write that she was sent to London in 1935 to train as a teacher and qualified in a year, though they do not name the college.[^2] See [Whitelands College](../Organisations/ORG-0021.md).

[^2]: Hakim Adi; Marika Sherwood, [*Pan-African History: Political Figures from Africa and the Diaspora since 1787*](../Sources/SRC-0133.md) (London: Routledge, 2003), 'Constance Cummings-John', p. 29.
