# Amy Ashwood Garvey: International African Service Bureau

*Amy Ashwood Garvey was among the founders of the International African Service Bureau in 1937 and its vice-president.*

[Amy Ashwood Garvey](../People/PER-0004.md) was among the founders of the [International African Service Bureau](../Organisations/ORG-0009.md) (IASB) in 1937.[^1] Høgsbjerg records her as its vice-president, with [George Padmore](../People/PER-0005.md) as chair and [C. L. R. James](../People/PER-0014.md) as editorial director.[^2][^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Founding date.** Høgsbjerg dates the launch to May 1937; Fryer writes that the Bureau replaced the IAFA in March 1937.[^3][^4]
