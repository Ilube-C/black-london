[Henry Sylvester Williams](../People/PER-0003.md) was admitted to [Gray's Inn](../Organisations/ORG-0004.md) in 1897, the year after he reached London, "to prepare for legal qualifications". He was called to the Bar in June 1902.[^1] Clarke writes that he "qualified and practised at Gray's Inn".[^2] His years as a law student were also the years in which he founded the [African Association](../Organisations/ORG-0001.md) and organised the [First Pan-African Conference](../Events/EVT-0001.md).[^3]
