The first International Book Fair of Radical Black and Third World Books was held in April 1982 at [Islington Town Hall](Atlas/Places/PLC-0077.md). Bunce and Field describe it as a "week-long literary festival" whose success "led to it becoming an annual event thereafter".[^1] It brought together the independent Black publishers and booksellers of London, among them [Jessica Huntley](Atlas/People/PER-0023.md)'s [Bogle-L'Ouverture Publications](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0030.md).[^1][^2]

## Planning

The fair was planned at a meeting at the Finsbury Park home of John La Rose, founder of New Beacon Books, on 18 January 1981. Those present included Jessica and Eric Huntley, who ran Bogle-L'Ouverture and the [Walter Rodney Bookshop](Atlas/Places/PLC-0076.md), and the activist Darcus Howe. They had met "to discuss the proposal to hold a Book Fair of Radical and Third World Books".[^1] In the early hours of that morning, a fire at a birthday party at 439 New Cross Road, Deptford, had killed thirteen young people aged between 14 and 22. The meeting broke up when the news arrived, and its participants went on to organise the response that led to the [Black People's Day of Action](Atlas/Events/EVT-0023.md).[^1]

## The Huntleys' part

The Huntley papers at the London Metropolitan Archives record Jessica Huntley as one of the fair's joint directors. Minutes of 1983–6 record her resignation from that role.[^2] An Eric Huntley notebook of 1983 mentions the second fair.[^2] The papers of Darcus Howe also include printed material from the fair for 1985–1989 and a file on "Caribbean in Crisis–International Book Fair, 1985".[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Dates.** The sources consulted give the month of the first fair, April 1982, but not its exact dates.
- **Programme.** The participants and programme of the first fair are not documented in the sources consulted.

## Connected people

- **[Jessica Huntley](Atlas/People/PER-0023.md)** — Jessica Huntley helped plan the first International Book Fair of Radical Black and Third World Books from January 1981 and became one of its joint directors.[^4]
