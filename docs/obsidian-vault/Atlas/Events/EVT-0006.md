On Thursday 11 July 1996 [Nelson Mandela](Atlas/People/PER-0002.md), the first President of democratic South Africa, addressed the members of both Houses of Parliament in [Westminster Hall](Atlas/Places/PLC-0004.md).[^1][^2] Parliament's own record of his visit notes that he "addressed both Houses of Parliament in Westminster Hall on 11 July 1996".[^3]

## The occasion

The Lord Chancellor's welcome explained the honour. Only at rare intervals, he said, did the two Houses invite a guest "to address both Houses of Parliament assembled in this Great Hall". He recalled the hall's history, from the oath sworn by Henry III in the thirteenth century to the trial of Sir Thomas More. He also noted Westminster's legislation on South Africa: the Act of 1909 that created the Union of South Africa, and the South Africa Act of 1995 that welcomed the country back into the Commonwealth.[^1] Mandela's daughter Zenani was welcomed with him.[^1]

## The speech

Mandela's address ([Address in Westminster Hall](Atlas/Works/WRK-0002.md)) set his presidency in two centuries of British involvement in southern Africa. "Perhaps the fact that we are here today", he said, "might serve to close a circle which is two hundred years old", dating Britain's arrival as a colonising power to 1795.[^1] He recalled that "eight decades ago" ANC leaders had come to these Houses to plead for equal treatment and been rebuffed. He said that his delegation returned "neither with pikes, nor with a desire for revenge". He named British figures whom he counted as friends of the struggle: William Wilberforce, Fenner Brockway, Harold Macmillan and Archbishop Trevor Huddleston.[^1] The Parliamentary Archives hold the text of the speeches by Mandela, the Lord Chancellor and the Speaker.[^2]

## Connections

In 1962, as a fugitive, Mandela had visited the Houses of Parliament as a sightseer, with [Oliver Tambo](Atlas/People/PER-0020.md) (see [Nelson Mandela's clandestine visit](Atlas/Events/EVT-0005.md)).[^4] During the same 1996 state visit he also went to Brixton (see [Visit to Brixton](Atlas/Events/EVT-0007.md)).[^5]
