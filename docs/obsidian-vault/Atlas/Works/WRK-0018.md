*The monthly paper of the West African National Secretariat, edited in London by Kwame Nkrumah, which first appeared in March 1946.*

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Date | 1946 |

*The New African* was the paper of the [West African National Secretariat](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0024.md) (WANS). [Kwame Nkrumah](Atlas/People/PER-0016.md) recalled raising fifty pounds so that the printers would produce three thousand copies of the first issue. It appeared in March 1946, subtitled "The Voice of the Awakened African", and sold out within hours at threepence a copy.[^1] He edited it with the Nigerian student Bankole Akpata, and later paid the £150 still owed to the printers.[^1][^2] Adi notes articles in French and others reprinted from the Moscow *New Times*.[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **How many issues.** Adi counts five issues, the last in July 1946.[^3] Sherwood finds no regular issues after mid-1946, though WANS sent a "special conference edition" to the Colonial Office in October 1947, of which no copy is known to survive.[^4] Nkrumah says only that publication stopped "after a short time" for lack of funds.[^2]
