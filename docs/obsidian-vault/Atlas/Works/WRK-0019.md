*Kwame Nkrumah's pamphlet against colonial rule, drafted in the United States and published in London in 1947.*

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Date | 1947 |

*Towards Colonial Freedom* is a pamphlet by [Kwame Nkrumah](Atlas/People/PER-0016.md). He recalled drafting it in the United States, publishing it only once he had scraped together the money in London. Its preface stated his "uncompromising opposition to all colonial policies".[^1] Sherwood dates its publication to 1947. The pamphlet bears no publisher's name and was printed by the Farleigh Press in Watford.[^2] Because that press belonged to the Communist Party, she infers that the Party may have paid for it.[^2] Bankole Akpata complained that Nkrumah had found money for the pamphlet but none for the [West African National Secretariat](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0024.md).[^2] Its appendices reprinted the Declaration to the Colonial Peoples of the World from the [Fifth Pan-African Congress](Atlas/Events/EVT-0004.md) and the Congress's resolutions on West Africa. Later editions dropped the resolutions.[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Later editions.** Adi and Sherwood list a Panaf Books edition of 1962; Sherwood's own notes cite a Heinemann edition of 1962.[^4][^5]
