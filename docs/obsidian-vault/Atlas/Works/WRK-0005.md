*Ignatius Sancho's first published collection of dance music, 'Composed by an African' and dedicated to the Duke of Buccleuch, published in London in the 1760s.*

[Ignatius Sancho](Atlas/People/PER-0007.md) published music as well as letters. Carretta identifies his first collection of country dances and minuets as *Minuets, Cotillons & Country Dances for the Violin, Mandolin, German Flute, & Harpsichord Composed by an African*. It was "Most Humbly Inscribed to his Grace Henry Duke of Buccleugh", the Montagus' son-in-law, and appeared in London in the 1760s.[^1] Later came *A Collection of New Songs, Composed by an African* (about 1769), dedicated to Mrs James Brudenell.[^2] In 1779 Sancho published *Twelve Country Dances for the Year 1779*, dedicated to Miss North. Its first set is "Lady Mary Montagus Reel", and its last is "Mungo's Delight".[^1][^3] The memoir prefixed to his *Letters* adds that a *Theory of Music* was "published, and dedicated to the Princess Royal". That work is now lost.[^4]
