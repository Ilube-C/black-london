## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Altheia Jones-LeCointe](Atlas/People/PER-0024.md): At the 1971 Mangrove Nine trial she was acquitted of the most serious charges (riot) but convicted of causing an affray and of assaulting police officers; sentenced to fifteen months' imprisonment, suspended for two years. — Home Office report 'The Trial of the Mangrove Nine', December 1971, para. 2: 'The trial ended on 15th December when five were acquitted and four convicted. Two, Rhodan GORDON and Althea LECOINTE were convicted of causing an affray and three, Althea LECOINTE, Anthony INNIS and Rupert BOYCE were convicted of assaulting Police officers. … GORDON and LECOINTE to fifteen months, all sentences were suspended for two years.'
