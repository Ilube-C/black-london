## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Ignatius Sancho](Atlas/People/PER-0007.md): Served the Duchess of Montagu as butler until her death, then the Duke as his valet, until gout ended his service towards the close of 1773. — p. x: after the duke's death in 1749 the duchess 'hired him as her butler'; p. xi: the new duke 'soon placed him about his person'; p. 255 n.: the name Montagu House transferred to the Privy Gardens residence
- [Ignatius Sancho](Atlas/People/PER-0007.md): Kept a grocer's shop with his wife after leaving the Duke's service towards the close of 1773. — p. xii: a grocery shop 'in early 1774 at 19 Charles Street, next to their new residence at 20 Charles Street'
- [Ignatius Sancho](Atlas/People/PER-0007.md): Voted in the 1774 and 1780 Westminster parliamentary elections. — p. xiv: as a male householder in Westminster he voted 'in 1774 and 1780, for the representatives of Westminster'
- [Ignatius Sancho](Atlas/People/PER-0007.md): Married Ann Osborne at St Margaret's, Westminster, on 17 December 1758. — p. 250, n. 14: married Ann Osborne 'on 17 December 1758 in St. Margaret's Church, Westminster'
- [Ignatius Sancho](Atlas/People/PER-0007.md): Authored letters published posthumously in 1782. — title verso: 'First published in Great Britain 1782'
