## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [William Cuffay](Atlas/People/PER-0009.md): Westminster's representative on the National Charter Association's metropolitan district council from 1841, and its treasurer from 1842. — p. 304: 'Westminster's representative on the metropolitan district council of the NCA from 1841, and its treasurer from 1842'
- [William Cuffay](Atlas/People/PER-0009.md): Delegate to the April 1848 National Chartist Convention and chair of the 'Demonstration Committee' for the 10 April meeting on Kennington Common. — p. 303: 'a London tailor and chair of the ‘Demonstration Committee’'; p. 304: 'When delegates assembled on 4 April, Cuffay quickly punctured Reynolds’s air of self-importance'
- [William Cuffay](Atlas/People/PER-0009.md): Arrested on the evening of 18 August 1848 at his lodging, 11 Hollins Street, Wardour Street, Soho, two days after the arrests at the Orange Tree (16 August). — p. 310: 'arrested while at home with Mary on 18 August'
- [William Cuffay](Atlas/People/PER-0009.md): Tried at the Central Criminal Court in the September 1848 session with William Lacey and Thomas Fay for feloniously compassing to levy war against the Queen; found guilty and sentenced to transportation for life. — p. 310: 'Cuffay was sentenced to transportation for life.'
