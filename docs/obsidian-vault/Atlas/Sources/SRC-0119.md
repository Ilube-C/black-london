## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [W. E. B. Du Bois](Atlas/People/PER-0001.md): Attended the 1900 First Pan-African Conference. — 'The First Pan-African Conference took place between Monday 23rd and Wednesday 25th July 1900. The American WEB Du Bois was among them ... the landmark event in Westminster Town Hall.'
- [W. E. B. Du Bois](Atlas/People/PER-0001.md): Chaired the committee for 'To the Nations of the World'. — 'WEB Du Bois was chairman of the committee tasked with composing this document'
- [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md): Birth disputed: earlier biographers gave 19 February 1869 in Arouca, Trinidad; Sherwood reports a Barbados birth registration dated 24 March 1867. — Gives Barbados, 1867 ('once upon a time in Barbados (1867 to be exact)'); supports the 1867 side only
- [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md): His family moved to Arouca, Trinidad, shortly after his birth, and he always identified himself as Trinidadian. — 'a baby boy was born whose family moved to Arouca in Trinidad and Tobago shortly afterwards. Henry Sylvester Williams would always identify himself as a Trini'
- [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md): His family moved from Barbados, where he was born, to Arouca in Trinidad shortly after his birth. — 'once upon a time in Barbados (1867 to be exact), a baby boy was born whose family moved to Arouca in Trinidad and Tobago shortly afterwards'
- [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md): Studied at King's College, London, from 1896: Afropean says law; Adi & Sherwood say he enrolled as an evening student taking Latin. — 'Williams arrived in London in to pursue studies in Law at Kings College in the 1890s'
- [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md): Admitted to Gray's Inn in 1897 (Adi & Sherwood); qualified and practised there (Afropean); called to the Bar in June 1902. — 'He qualified and practised at Gray's Inn.'
- [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md): Organised the 1900 Pan-African Conference. — 'Williams, with his extensive list of contacts in all corners of the Black Atlantic, was the principal organiser.'
- [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md): Attended 1900 Conference alongside Du Bois. — 'Williams ... was the principal organiser'; 'The American WEB Du Bois was among them'; Du Bois: 'Great credit is due our genial secretary, Mr. H. Sylvester Williams, for these social functions.'
- [Samuel Coleridge-Taylor](Atlas/People/PER-0010.md): Participated in the 1900 First Pan-African Conference. — 'Samuel Coleridge Taylor ... provided the musical offering and later joined the committee.'
- [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md): Died on 26 March 1911 (Adi & Sherwood); in Port of Spain, aged 44 (Afropean). — 'Henry Sylvester Williams died in 1911 in Port of Spain aged only 44.'
