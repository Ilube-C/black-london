## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [W. E. B. Du Bois](Atlas/People/PER-0001.md): Holt says that Du Bois co-chaired the opening session; Padmore's proceedings put him alone in the chair on 16 October. — p. 32: first session of 16 October, 'Chairman: Dr. W. E. BURGHARDT DU BOIS (U.S.A.)'; p. 37: second session, the same
- [Amy Ashwood Garvey](Atlas/People/PER-0004.md): Padmore records that she chaired the opening session of 15 October; Holt says that she co-chaired it with Du Bois. — p. 27: opening session of 15 October, 'Chairman: Mrs. AMY ASHWOOD GARVEY (Jamaica)'
