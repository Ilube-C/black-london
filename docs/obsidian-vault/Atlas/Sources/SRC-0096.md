## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Olaudah Equiano](Atlas/People/PER-0006.md): Birthplace disputed between Essaka, Igboland (autobiography) and South Carolina (naval and baptismal records). — Vol. I, ch. I: 'I was born, in the year 1745, in a charming fruitful vale, named Essaka'
- [Olaudah Equiano](Atlas/People/PER-0006.md): Baptised at St Margaret's, Westminster, on 9 February 1759. — Vol. I, ch. IV: 'I was baptized in St. Margaret's church, Westminster, in February 1759'
- [Olaudah Equiano](Atlas/People/PER-0006.md): Title page of the 1789 first edition gives the author's address as No. 10, Union-Street, Middlesex Hospital. — Title page: 'Printed for and sold by the Author, No. 10, Union-Street, Middlesex Hospital'; dedication dated 'Union-Street, Mary-le-bone, March 24, 1789'
- [Olaudah Equiano](Atlas/People/PER-0006.md): Authored and self-published The Interesting Narrative in 1789. — Title page: 'WRITTEN BY HIMSELF' … 'Printed for and sold by the Author'
