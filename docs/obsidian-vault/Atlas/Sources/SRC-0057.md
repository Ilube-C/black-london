## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Jessica Huntley](Atlas/People/PER-0023.md): Operated the bookshop at 5a Chignell Place, West Ealing (renamed the Walter Rodney Bookshop in 1980) — LMA/4463/B/09/01/002: 'Minutes of meetings held at Walter Rodney Bookshop, 5a Chignell Place, Ealing' (1986)
- [Jessica Huntley](Atlas/People/PER-0023.md): On 18 January 1981 attended the meeting at John La Rose's home in Finsbury Park called to discuss holding the Book Fair, first held in April 1982; later one of the fair's joint directors. — LMA/4463/B/02/01/009: minutes of 1983–6 recording Jessica Huntley's resignation as one of the Book Fair's joint directors
