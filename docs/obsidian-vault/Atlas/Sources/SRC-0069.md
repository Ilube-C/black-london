## Passages recorded from this source

These are the passages, page references and short quotations recorded from this source as evidence for the atlas's data.

- [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md): Birth disputed: earlier biographers gave 19 February 1869 in Arouca, Trinidad; Sherwood reports a Barbados birth registration dated 24 March 1867. — p. 5: 'Henry Sylvester Williams was not born on 19 February 1869 in Arouca, as reported by previous biographers. Ronald Noel has located the registration of his birth, dated 24 March 1867, in Barbados.'
- [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md): His family moved from Barbados, where he was born, to Arouca in Trinidad shortly after his birth. — p. 5: 'He was the eldest son of Henry Bishop and Elizabeth Williams, who emigrated to Trinidad from Barbados at an unknown date.'
- [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md): Organised the 1900 Pan-African Conference. — p. xiii: 'he constantly interrupted his training as a barrister to form the African Association which immediately embarked on political activities ... the Association called the world's first Pan-African Conference'
- [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md): Elected to Marylebone Council in 1906. — p. xiii: 'one of the first two elected Black borough councillors in London'; p. 13: his statement to The Marylebone Mercury 'in 1906 … on his election as a councillor in that London borough'
