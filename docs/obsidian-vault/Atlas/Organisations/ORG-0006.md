*The London borough council to which Henry Sylvester Williams was elected in November 1906 as a Progressive for the Church Street ward.*

St Marylebone Borough Council was the local authority for Marylebone. In November 1906 [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md) won a seat on it as a "Progressive", meaning a Liberal-Labour candidate. Fryer records that he polled 701 votes in the Church Street ward.[^1] Adi and Sherwood note that he stood with the support of the Workers' Union. They add that his attendance at the council was irregular.[^2] Sherwood counts him as "one of the first two elected Black borough councillors in London".[^3] Westminster City Council calls him the first Black councillor in Westminster. It has put up a green plaque at [38 Church Street](Atlas/Places/PLC-0009.md), which it describes as his former home.[^4]
