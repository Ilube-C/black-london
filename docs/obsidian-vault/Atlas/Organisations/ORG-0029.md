*The Institute of International Visual Arts and Autograph ABP (the Association of Black Photographers), the two arts organisations, both chaired by Stuart Hall, that commissioned Rivington Place in Shoreditch.*

Iniva, the Institute of International Visual Arts, and Autograph ABP, the Association of Black Photographers, are arts organisations. Martin Jacques's biography of [Stuart Hall](Atlas/People/PER-0022.md) records that Hall chaired both. They were the two bodies that commissioned [Rivington Place](Atlas/Places/PLC-0074.md) in Shoreditch, and Jacques calls Hall the central figure in its creation. The building opened in 2007.[^1] The atlas records the two organisations together because the source consulted presents them jointly as the commissioners of Rivington Place. Their separate histories are not covered in the sources consulted.
