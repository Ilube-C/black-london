*The London school where Paul Robeson enrolled to study Swahili and phonetics in 1934, and where Jomo Kenyatta worked as a language informant.*

The School of Oriental Studies, now SOAS University of London, holds the admission form of [Paul Robeson](Atlas/People/PER-0019.md). Its special collections describe it as "the original admission form of Paul Robeson to study Swahili and Phonetics at SOAS in 1934".[^1] Berman records that [Jomo Kenyatta](Atlas/People/PER-0017.md) earned his main income, after his return from Russia, as a linguistic informant at University College and SOAS.[^2] The sources consulted do not show the two men there together. The school is recorded at [the School of Oriental Studies](Atlas/Places/PLC-0065.md).

## Connected people

- **[Paul Robeson](Atlas/People/PER-0019.md)** — Paul Robeson enrolled at the School of Oriental Studies in 1934 to study Swahili and phonetics.[^3]
