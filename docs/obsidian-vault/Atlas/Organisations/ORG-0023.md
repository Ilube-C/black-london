*The London college where Jomo Kenyatta studied social anthropology under Bronisław Malinowski from 1935, and where Kwame Nkrumah attended lectures in 1945.*

The London School of Economics appears in the atlas through two future heads of state.

- **[Jomo Kenyatta](Atlas/People/PER-0017.md)** became the anthropologist Bronisław Malinowski's personal student in the spring of 1935. That autumn he enrolled for the three-year postgraduate diploma in social anthropology, and he submitted his thesis at the end of 1937.[^1] In his introduction to [*Facing Mount Kenya*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0020.md), Malinowski writes that Kenyatta had been "a member of my discussion class at the London School of Economics" for several years.[^2]
- **[Kwame Nkrumah](Atlas/People/PER-0016.md)** arranged to attend lectures at the LSE on arriving in London in 1945. There he met Harold Laski, who "lectured there on political science".[^3]

Its site is recorded under [the London School of Economics](Atlas/Places/PLC-0055.md).
