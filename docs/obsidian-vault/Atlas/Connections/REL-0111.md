[Kwame Nkrumah](Atlas/People/PER-0016.md) was a founder and the general secretary of the [West African National Secretariat](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0024.md) (WANS), formed in London at the end of 1945.[^1][^2] He recalled that he "was offered and accepted the secretaryship" and that its office was at [94 Gray's Inn Road](Atlas/Places/PLC-0056.md).[^3] A fellow member remembered him as "the moving spirit from the beginning".[^4] He also edited its paper, [*The New African*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0018.md).[^5]
