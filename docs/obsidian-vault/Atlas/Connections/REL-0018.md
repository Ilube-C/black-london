The Nigerian Progress Union was founded by thirteen Nigerian students in London in July 1924. Hakim Adi writes that it was formed "largely due to [Amy Ashwood Garvey](Atlas/People/PER-0004.md)'s enthusiasm".[^1] Rhoda Reddock likewise records that she helped organise it.[^2] Its leading figure was [Ladipo Solanke](Atlas/People/PER-0011.md) (see [Solanke and Ashwood Garvey](Atlas/Connections/REL-0042.md)).[^1] The Union is described under [Nigerian Progress Union](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0007.md).
