[Samuel Coleridge-Taylor](Atlas/People/PER-0010.md) took part in the [First Pan-African Conference](Atlas/Events/EVT-0001.md) at [Westminster Town Hall](Atlas/Places/PLC-0001.md) in July 1900. Fryer writes that he "took charge of the musical side of the programme" and was elected to the executive committee of the Pan-African Association formed there.[^1] Clarke likewise records that he "provided the musical offering and later joined the committee".[^2] Richards calls him the youngest delegate.[^3]
