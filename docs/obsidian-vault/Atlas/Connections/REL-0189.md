On 18 January 1981 [Jessica Huntley](Atlas/People/PER-0023.md) and Eric Huntley met John La Rose and Darcus Howe at La Rose's home in Finsbury Park "to discuss the proposal to hold a Book Fair of Radical and Third World Books".[^1] The [first fair](Atlas/Events/EVT-0022.md) was held at [Islington Town Hall](Atlas/Places/PLC-0077.md) in April 1982.[^1] The Huntley papers record Jessica Huntley as one of the fair's joint directors, and minutes of 1983–6 record her resignation from that role.[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **Spelling.** Bunce and Field's text, as consulted, names "Jessica and Erica [sic] Huntley".[^1]
