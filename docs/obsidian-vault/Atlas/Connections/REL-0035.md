*Mary Prince was briefly called as a witness in the libel trial Pringle v. Cadell in the Court of Common Pleas on 21 February 1833.*

In [*Pringle v. Cadell*](Atlas/Events/EVT-0010.md), heard in the Court of Common Pleas on 21 February 1833, Thomas Pringle sued the London publisher of an article attacking [*The History of Mary Prince*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0006.md).[^1] Lambert writes that [Mary Prince](Atlas/People/PER-0008.md) "was briefly called as witness, but only to confirm that she had dictated her account for Pringle and she was not cross-examined".[^2] Her full testimony came in the second trial, [*Wood v. Pringle*](Atlas/Events/EVT-0011.md), six days later.[^3]
