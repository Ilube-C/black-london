[Jessica Huntley](Atlas/People/PER-0023.md) and her husband Eric Huntley founded [Bogle-L'Ouverture Publications](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0030.md). The British Library writes that it "was founded in 1968 by two Guyanese anti-colonial nationalist party leaders, Jessica and Eric Huntley".[^1] The firm grew out of their campaign for the historian Walter Rodney, and its first book was his [*The Groundings with My Brothers*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0026.md) (1969).[^2][^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Founding year.** The British Library gives 1968. London Remembers gives both 1968 and 1969.[^1][^4]
