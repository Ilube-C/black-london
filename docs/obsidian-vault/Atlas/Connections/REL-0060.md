*Constance Cummings-John trained as a teacher at Whitelands College, Putney, after coming to England in 1935.*

Sierra Leone Web records that [Constance Cummings-John](Atlas/People/PER-0015.md) went to England at the age of seventeen "and entered Whitelands College in Putney, where she trained as a teacher".[^1] Adi and Sherwood write that she was sent to London in 1935 to train as a teacher and qualified in a year, though they do not name the college.[^2] See [Whitelands College](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0021.md).
