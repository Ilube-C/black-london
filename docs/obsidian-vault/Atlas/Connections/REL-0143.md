*Nelson Mandela and Oliver Tambo met in London in June 1962, when Mandela visited the city during his months abroad.*

In June 1962 [Nelson Mandela](Atlas/People/PER-0002.md) spent about ten days in London, where [Oliver Tambo](Atlas/People/PER-0020.md) was leading the ANC's mission abroad (see [Mandela's visit](Atlas/Events/EVT-0005.md)).[^1] Mandela's own account shows them together: Tambo arranged his meeting with David Astor, and the two men saw Westminster Abbey, Big Ben and the Houses of Parliament with the writer Mary Benson.[^1] Callinicos writes that "Then Mandela arrived" after Tambo's return to London.[^2] Adi and Sherwood also record the visit.[^3]
