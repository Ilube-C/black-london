[Jomo Kenyatta](Atlas/People/PER-0017.md) lodged at [95 Cambridge Street](Atlas/Places/PLC-0057.md), Pimlico, during two of his stays in London. English Heritage, whose blue plaque marks the house, writes that he stayed "for a short spell in 1930 and for a further four years in 1933–7".[^1] Murray-Brown writes that on his return from Russia he "returned to his old digs at 95 Cambridge Street" and persuaded his landlady, Mrs Hocken, to take him in again.[^2]
