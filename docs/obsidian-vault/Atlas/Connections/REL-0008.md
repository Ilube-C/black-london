On 11 July 1996 [Nelson Mandela](Atlas/People/PER-0002.md), President of South Africa, addressed the members of both Houses of Parliament assembled in [Westminster Hall](Atlas/Places/PLC-0004.md) (see [the address](Atlas/Events/EVT-0006.md)).[^1][^2] The Lord Chancellor welcomed him and the Speaker of the House of Commons thanked him.[^3] Parliament's own record notes that he "addressed both Houses of Parliament in Westminster Hall on 11 July 1996".[^4] The text of his speech is described under [Address in Westminster Hall](Atlas/Works/WRK-0002.md).

[^3]: UK Parliament, [*The President of The Republic of South Africa Mr Nelson Mandela: Speeches in Westminster Hall, 11 July 1996*](Atlas/Sources/SRC-0005.md) (UK Parliament, 1996-07-11), p. 2 (speech of the Lord Chancellor) and p. 8 (speech of the Speaker).
