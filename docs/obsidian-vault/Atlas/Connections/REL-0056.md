[C. L. R. James](Atlas/People/PER-0014.md) founded and chaired the [International African Friends of Abyssinia](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0008.md) (IAFA). Høgsbjerg describes how James and [Amy Ashwood Garvey](Atlas/People/PER-0004.md) revived an earlier committee in 1935, which became the IAFA, and "James became chair".[^1] Høgsbjerg dates his chairmanship to 1935–36.[^2] Adi and Sherwood write that "Under the leadership of James" the group was formed in 1935.[^3] The IAFA campaigned against the Italian invasion of Ethiopia.[^4]
