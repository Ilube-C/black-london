*Oliver Tambo used the ANC's London mission at 28 Penton Street, holding early-morning meetings there when he was in London.*

The ANC's headquarters in Britain was at [28 Penton Street](Atlas/Places/PLC-0068.md), Islington, by 1982.[^1] The Anti-Apartheid Movement's archives committee recorded that [Oliver Tambo](Atlas/People/PER-0020.md), President of the ANC, "was known, whilst in London, to hold early morning meetings at the ANC Mission in Penton Street".[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **Dates.** The sources consulted do not say when the ANC moved to Penton Street or in which years Tambo met there.

[^2]: AAM Archives Committee (presented by Lord Hughes), [*Submission to the Truth and Reconciliation Commission on South Africa's Illegal and Covert Activities in the United Kingdom*](Atlas/Sources/SRC-0150.md) (TRC seminar, Cape Town, 1997-11-10), section 5(a), p. 15.
