[Una Marson](Atlas/People/PER-0013.md) worked for the [League of Coloured Peoples](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0019.md) during her first stay in England, from 1932 to 1936. Jarrett-Macauley writes that in autumn 1933 she "became the League's unpaid assistant secretary".[^1] She also edited its journal, [*The Keys*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0010.md).[^2] She mounted the League's performance of her play [*At What a Price*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0011.md) in November 1933.[^3] Busby describes her as "secretary of the League of Coloured Peoples in London".[^4]

## Evidence notes

- **Title.** Busby calls her the League's secretary; Jarrett-Macauley calls her its unpaid assistant secretary.[^4][^1]
