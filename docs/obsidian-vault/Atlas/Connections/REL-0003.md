[Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md) and [W. E. B. Du Bois](Atlas/People/PER-0001.md) both took part in the [First Pan-African Conference](Atlas/Events/EVT-0001.md) in London in July 1900. Williams was its principal organiser and secretary. Du Bois attended as a delegate.[^1][^2] Both men signed the conference's address, ["To the Nations of the World"](Atlas/Works/WRK-0001.md).[^3] In his later account of the conference, Du Bois wrote: "Great credit is due our genial secretary, Mr. H. Sylvester Williams, for these social functions."[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **What is documented.** The sources consulted place both men at the conference, show them signing the same address and record Du Bois's praise of Williams. They do not describe a meeting or conversation between the two men, so none is stated here.
