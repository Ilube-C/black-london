In the September 1848 session of the [Central Criminal Court](Atlas/Places/PLC-0028.md), [William Cuffay](Atlas/People/PER-0009.md) was tried with William Lacey and Thomas Fay (see [the trial](Atlas/Events/EVT-0014.md)). The indictment charged that they "feloniously did compass, imagine, devise, and intend, to levy war against the Queen".[^1] The jury found him guilty. The record gives his age as 60 and his sentence as transportation for life.[^1][^2] The trial followed the conspiracy uncovered by the police raid on the [Orange Tree](Atlas/Events/EVT-0013.md) in August.[^3]
