[Amy Ashwood Garvey](Atlas/People/PER-0004.md) lived at [1 Bassett Road](Atlas/Places/PLC-0011.md), Ladbroke Grove, in the late 1950s. After the Notting Hill riots of 1958, her Association for the Advancement of Coloured People met there, with [Claudia Jones](Atlas/People/PER-0018.md) as its general secretary.[^1] The Jamaica Information Service reported that a plaque honouring her was unveiled at the house "on November 5" 2009.[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **The plaque today.** The plaque is known from a contemporary news report. Its present state was not checked against an official record.
