[Bogle-L'Ouverture](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0030.md)'s bookshop opened in October 1975 in a former dress shop at [5a Chignell Place](Atlas/Places/PLC-0076.md), West Ealing. It was renamed the Walter Rodney Bookshop in 1980.[^1] [Jessica Huntley](Atlas/People/PER-0023.md) and Eric Huntley ran it through years of racist attacks, and helped form Book Shop Joint Action in response.[^1][^2] Minutes of meetings held at the shop in 1986 survive in the Huntley papers.[^2] Oates writes that the business closed in March 1991.[^1]
