*Paul Robeson played Othello at the Savoy Theatre, opening on 19 May 1930.*

On the evening of 19 May 1930 [Paul Robeson](Atlas/People/PER-0019.md) opened as Othello at the [Savoy Theatre](Atlas/Places/PLC-0063.md) in London, in a production presented by the actor-manager Maurice Browne (see [the opening night](Atlas/Events/EVT-0020.md)).[^1] Morrison writes that Robeson "was the first actor of African descent to appear in an extended-run Shakespearean production at a leading West End venue".[^2] English Heritage notes that the production "won many plaudits".[^3]
