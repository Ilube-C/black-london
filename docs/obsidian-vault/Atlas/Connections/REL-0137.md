[Paul Robeson](Atlas/People/PER-0019.md) enrolled at the [School of Oriental Studies](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0026.md), now SOAS University of London, in 1934. SOAS holds "the original admission form of Paul Robeson to study Swahili and Phonetics at SOAS in 1934".[^1] The school was then at [Finsbury Circus](Atlas/Places/PLC-0065.md) in the City of London.[^2]
