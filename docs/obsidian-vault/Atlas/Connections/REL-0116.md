[Jomo Kenyatta](Atlas/People/PER-0017.md) studied at the [London School of Economics](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0023.md). Berman writes that by the spring of 1935 he was Bronisław Malinowski's personal student. That autumn he enrolled for the three-year postgraduate diploma in social anthropology.[^1] His thesis, submitted at the end of 1937, became [*Facing Mount Kenya*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0020.md).[^2] Malinowski wrote that "For several years past Mr. Kenyatta has been a member of my discussion class at the London School of Economics".[^3]
