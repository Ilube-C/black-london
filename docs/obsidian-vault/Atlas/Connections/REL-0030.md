[Ignatius Sancho](Atlas/People/PER-0007.md) spent his working life in the service of the Montagu family. The memoir prefixed to his *Letters* records that after the second Duke of Montagu died in 1749, the Duchess took Sancho into her household as butler.[^1] The memoir adds that the new Duke "soon placed him about his person", and Carretta describes him as the Duke's valet.[^1][^2] The family's residence in the Privy Gardens, Whitehall, took the name [Montagu House](Atlas/Places/PLC-0019.md).[^2] Gout ended his service towards the close of 1773.[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **Which house and when.** The sources consulted do not say which years Sancho spent at the Whitehall house, as distinct from the family's other residences.
