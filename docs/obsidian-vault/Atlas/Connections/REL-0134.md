English Heritage records that "At the peak of his fame in 1929–30" [Paul Robeson](Atlas/People/PER-0019.md) lived at [The Chestnuts](Atlas/Places/PLC-0064.md), 1 Branch Hill, Hampstead. Its blue plaque gives the dates 1929–1930.[^1] These were the months between his success in *Show Boat* at [Drury Lane](Atlas/Places/PLC-0062.md) in 1928 and his first *Othello* at the [Savoy Theatre](Atlas/Places/PLC-0063.md) in 1930.[^2]
