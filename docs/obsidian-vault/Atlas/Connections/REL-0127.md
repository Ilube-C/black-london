[Claudia Jones](Atlas/People/PER-0018.md) organised the [Caribbean Carnival](Atlas/Events/EVT-0019.md) held at [St Pancras Town Hall](Atlas/Places/PLC-0059.md) on Friday 30 January 1959.[^1][^2] Her paper, the [*West Indian Gazette*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0022.md), sponsored it, and it was an indoor evening of cabaret, music and dance.[^1] A former *Gazette* reporter later insisted that the Carnival "started in the room above Theo Campbell's record shop in 250 Brixton Road", the paper's office.[^3]
