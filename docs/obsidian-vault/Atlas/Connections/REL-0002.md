[W. E. B. Du Bois](Atlas/People/PER-0001.md) chaired the committee that drafted ["To the Nations of the World"](Atlas/Works/WRK-0001.md), the public address of the [First Pan-African Conference](Atlas/Events/EVT-0001.md) of July 1900.[^1][^2] The conference adopted it unanimously.[^3] A copy in the Du Bois Papers is signed by Du Bois with Alexander Walters, [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md) and Henry B. Brown.[^4] Fryer notes that Du Bois later reused its phrase about "the problem of the colour-line" in *The Souls of Black Folk* (1903).[^3]
