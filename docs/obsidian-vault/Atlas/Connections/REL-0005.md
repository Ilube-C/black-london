[W. E. B. Du Bois](Atlas/People/PER-0001.md) and [George Padmore](Atlas/People/PER-0005.md) worked together on the [Fifth Pan-African Congress](Atlas/Events/EVT-0004.md) in Manchester in October 1945. Thomas Holt writes that Du Bois "joined Garvey's widow, Amy Jacques Garvey, and George Padmore" in sponsoring it.[^1] Padmore's published report shows Padmore proposing the Congress and opening the session of 16 October. Du Bois, elected International President, chaired the sessions of 16, 17 and 18 October.[^2] Padmore edited the report afterwards.[^2]
