Westminster City Council commemorates [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md) with a green plaque at [38 Church Street](Atlas/Places/PLC-0009.md), Marylebone, which it describes as his former home.[^1] The council's report calls him the first Black councillor in Westminster. He had been elected for the Church Street ward of [St Marylebone Borough Council](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0006.md) in 1906.[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **The house.** The council describes the address as his former home. The sources consulted do not give the years he lived there.
