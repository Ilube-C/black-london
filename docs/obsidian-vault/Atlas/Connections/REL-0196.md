*Jomo Kenyatta and Paul Robeson met during the filming of Sanders of the River in 1934–35 and became better acquainted, visiting each other's flats.*

[Jomo Kenyatta](Atlas/People/PER-0017.md) worked as an extra on [*Sanders of the River*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0021.md), the film in which [Paul Robeson](Atlas/People/PER-0019.md) starred.[^1] Murray-Brown calls it "a strange accident that brought Kenyatta and Robeson together". He writes that "They became better acquainted with each other", that Robeson gave Kenyatta records and a gramophone, and that "They visited each other's flats". Robeson's was a set of rooms off the Strand, and Kenyatta's the bed-sitting room that his landlady Mrs Hocken kept tidy.[^2] Murray-Brown elsewhere places Mrs Hocken's house at [95 Cambridge Street](Atlas/Places/PLC-0057.md).[^3]
