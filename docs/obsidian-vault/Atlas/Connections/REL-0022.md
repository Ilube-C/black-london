[Amy Ashwood Garvey](Atlas/People/PER-0004.md) and [George Padmore](Atlas/People/PER-0005.md) served together in two London organisations. In the [International African Friends of Abyssinia](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0008.md), formed in 1935, Ashwood Garvey was honorary treasurer and Padmore sat on the executive.[^1] In the [International African Service Bureau](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0009.md), founded in 1937, Padmore was chair and Ashwood Garvey vice-president.[^2][^3] Reddock records her collaboration with Padmore and James in forming the Bureau.[^4]
