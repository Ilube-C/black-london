[C. L. R. James](Atlas/People/PER-0014.md) and [Jomo Kenyatta](Atlas/People/PER-0017.md) served together as officers of the [International African Friends of Abyssinia](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0008.md) in 1935, James as chairman and Kenyatta as honorary secretary.[^1] Berman writes that James "introduced Kenyatta to his publisher, Frederic Warburg", who agreed to publish [*Facing Mount Kenya*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0020.md).[^2] James reviewed the book as "The Voice of Africa" in [*International African Opinion*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0013.md) in August 1938. Høgsbjerg describes their relationship as "fraternal but difficult".[^3]
