*Claudia Jones founded the West Indian Gazette in London in 1958 and published and edited it.*

[Claudia Jones](Atlas/People/PER-0018.md) founded the [*West Indian Gazette*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0022.md) in 1958.[^1] She set up the Coloured People's Publishing Company that owned it, and she edited the paper.[^2] Adi and Sherwood describe it as "A monthly paper that covered British and world political, economic and cultural news" which "was also a campaigning tool".[^3] In 1964 Jones described it to American readers as a paper that had "campaigned vigorously on issues facing West Indians and other coloured people".[^4]
