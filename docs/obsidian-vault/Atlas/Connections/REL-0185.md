[Stuart Hall](Atlas/People/PER-0022.md) was the first editor of [*New Left Review*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0025.md), formed in January 1960 from *Universities and Left Review* and *The New Reasoner*.[^1] The Stuart Hall Foundation's bibliography lists him as editor of *Universities and Left Review* in 1957–1959 and of *New Left Review* in 1960–1961.[^2] The Bishopsgate Institute records that Perry Anderson succeeded him in 1962.[^1] In his memoir Hall recalls being asked to take the post: "So they asked me and I stupidly said 'Yes'."[^3]
