*Amy Ashwood Garvey and Claudia Jones were friends and colleagues in London; Jones was general secretary of Ashwood Garvey's Association for the Advancement of Coloured People, founded in 1958.*

Sherwood counts [Amy Ashwood Garvey](Atlas/People/PER-0004.md) among [Claudia Jones](Atlas/People/PER-0018.md)'s "earliest friendships in Britain".[^1] After the Notting Hill riots of 1958, Ashwood Garvey founded the Association for the Advancement of Coloured People. By September 1958 it was holding a meeting at her home at [1 Bassett Road](Atlas/Places/PLC-0011.md). Sherwood writes that Jones, "clearly a friend and colleague of Amy's", was its general secretary.[^2] Though Ashwood Garvey was often abroad, "the two women retained a friendship", as letters and telegrams of 1964 show.[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Earlier acquaintance.** Sherwood suggests that the two women may have met in the United States, but states that this is not known.[^1]
