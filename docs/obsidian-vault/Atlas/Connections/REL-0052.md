*Una Marson lodged with Harold Moody's family at 164 Queen's Road, Peckham, from July 1932 until spring 1935.*

[Una Marson](Atlas/People/PER-0013.md) arrived in England in July 1932. Jarrett-Macauley records that she "found lodgings at 164 Queen's Road, Peckham", the home of [Harold Moody](Atlas/People/PER-0012.md) and his family.[^1] She stayed until spring 1935, when she "left the Moodys' busy household".[^2] While she lived there she edited the [League of Coloured Peoples](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0019.md)' journal, [*The Keys*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0010.md) (see [Marson and Moody](Atlas/Connections/REL-0043.md)).[^3]
