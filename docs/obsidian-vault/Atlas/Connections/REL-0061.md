*Constance Cummings-John took part in the League of Coloured Peoples while training as a teacher in London in 1935–36.*

While training as a teacher in London from 1935, [Constance Cummings-John](Atlas/People/PER-0015.md) took part in two of the main Black organisations in the city. Adi and Sherwood name "the West African Students' Union led by LADIPO SOLANKE and the League of Coloured People under the leadership of Dr HAROLD MOODY".[^1] Sierra Leone Web likewise records that she took part in the activities of the WASU and the [League of Coloured Peoples](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0019.md).[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **Role.** The sources consulted describe her involvement but do not give her a post in the League.
