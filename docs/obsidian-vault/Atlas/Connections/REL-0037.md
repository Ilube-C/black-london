[William Cuffay](Atlas/People/PER-0009.md), a London tailor, was a delegate to the National Convention that assembled on 4 April 1848 to present the Chartist petition.[^1][^2] He chaired the "Demonstration Committee" that organised the processions for the mass meeting on Kennington Common on 10 April (see [the Convention and demonstration](Atlas/Events/EVT-0012.md)).[^1] Chase writes that when the delegates assembled, Cuffay "quickly punctured Reynolds's air of self-importance".[^2]
