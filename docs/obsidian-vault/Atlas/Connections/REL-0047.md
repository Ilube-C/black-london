*Ladipo Solanke was the first warden of Africa House, the West African Students' Union hostel opened on Camden Road in 1933.*

[Ladipo Solanke](Atlas/People/PER-0011.md) raised the money for the [West African Students' Union](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0018.md)'s first hostel on a tour of West Africa between 1929 and 1932.[^1] Adi records that *Wasu* reported Solanke and Olu Obisanya, "the future Mrs Solanke", preparing the hostel, which "officially opened on 9 March 1933".[^2] When [Africa House](Atlas/Places/PLC-0035.md) opened on Camden Road, Solanke became its first warden. Adi and Sherwood write that he and his wife "continued to run the WASU hostel until the late-1940s".[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **Residence.** The sources consulted record Solanke as warden. They do not say whether he lived in the Camden Road building.
- **Address.** The street number 62 comes from the address printed in WASU's journal in January 1933.[^3]
