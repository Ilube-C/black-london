[Ladipo Solanke](Atlas/People/PER-0011.md) was a founder of the [West African Students' Union](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0018.md) (WASU). Adi and Sherwood write that "In 1925 he and Dr Herbert Bankole-Bright ... founded the West African Students' Union" and that Solanke became its first secretary-general.[^1] Adi dates the founding meeting to August 1925 and records that Solanke held the post for twenty-five years.[^2] Fryer describes the founders as twenty-one law students who later became prominent in West Africa.[^3] Solanke also ran the Union's hostel (see [Solanke and Africa House](Atlas/Connections/REL-0047.md)).
