*Stuart Hall took part in anti-racist work in Notting Hill after the riots of 1958, through the New Left's Universities and Left Review Club.*

[Stuart Hall](Atlas/People/PER-0022.md) writes that "by way of the New Left networks I became active in anti-racist work in Notting Hill".[^1] After the anti-Black riots of 1958, he records, the Universities and Left Review Club worked with local Black activists, churches and the local Labour Party "to put together local black and white tenants' defence associations".[^1] See [Notting Hill](Atlas/Places/PLC-0072.md).
