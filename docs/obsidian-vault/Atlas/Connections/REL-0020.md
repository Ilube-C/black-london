[Amy Ashwood Garvey](Atlas/People/PER-0004.md) helped found the [International African Friends of Abyssinia](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0008.md) (IAFA) in 1935, in opposition to Mussolini's war on Ethiopia. [C. L. R. James](Atlas/People/PER-0014.md) recalled that they both "felt that there ought to be an opposition".[^1] Together they revived an existing committee, which became the IAFA.[^1] Padmore's list of its officers names Ashwood Garvey as honorary treasurer.[^2] Adi and Sherwood also record her as a founder member and its treasurer.[^3]
