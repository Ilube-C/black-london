[Altheia Jones-LeCointe](Atlas/People/PER-0024.md) was one of the nine defendants in the [Mangrove Nine trial](Atlas/Events/EVT-0025.md), which began in Court 1 of the [Old Bailey](Atlas/Places/PLC-0028.md) on 5 October 1971.[^1][^2] She and Darcus Howe "took the unusual step of defending themselves at the trial".[^3] All nine were acquitted of the principal charge of incitement to riot.[^1] A Home Office report of December 1971 names her among the four convicted on lesser charges of affray and assault.[^4]

## Evidence notes

- **Outcome.** The National Archives writes that all nine "were acquitted of the most serious charges". The Open University records acquittals on 23 of the 32 charges.[^2][^5]
