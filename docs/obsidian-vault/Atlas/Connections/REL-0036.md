[William Cuffay](Atlas/People/PER-0009.md) served the [National Charter Association](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0014.md) (NCA) through its London district council. Chase writes that he was "Westminster's representative on the metropolitan district council of the NCA from 1841, and its treasurer from 1842".[^1] Fryer records that in 1841 the Westminster Chartists sent him to represent them on the Metropolitan Delegate Council. In 1842, after the arrest of the national leaders, the council appointed him president of an interim executive.[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **The 1842 executive.** Fryer records Cuffay's appointment as president of the interim executive. Chase writes that he "helped rescue the national executive when it faced crisis late in 1842".[^2][^1]
