In November 1906 [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md) won a seat on [St Marylebone Borough Council](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0006.md) as a "Progressive". Fryer records that he polled 701 votes in the Church Street ward.[^1] Adi and Sherwood note that he stood with the support of the Workers' Union.[^2] Sherwood counts him as "one of the first two elected Black borough councillors in London".[^3] Westminster City Council calls him the first Black councillor in Westminster and marks his former home at [38 Church Street](Atlas/Places/PLC-0009.md) (see [the plaque](Atlas/Connections/REL-0017.md)).[^4]
