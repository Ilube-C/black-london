*George Padmore and Jomo Kenyatta worked together from 1930, first in Padmore's Comintern network in Germany and Moscow, then in London's Pan-African organisations of the 1930s.*

[George Padmore](Atlas/People/PER-0005.md) and [Jomo Kenyatta](Atlas/People/PER-0017.md) worked together over fifteen years. Murray-Brown records Kenyatta's name on the provisional executive of the International Trade Union Committee of Negro Workers at its Hamburg conference of July 1930, which Padmore helped organise.[^1] In 1932, he writes, Kenyatta's "answer was to join Padmore in Germany", and Padmore later "had taken him to Moscow".[^2] In London, Kenyatta was honorary secretary of the [International African Friends of Abyssinia](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0008.md), on whose executive Padmore sat.[^3] Fryer records that Kenyatta worked closely with Padmore and others in founding the [International African Service Bureau](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0009.md).[^4] Berman names Padmore among the Bureau colleagues who found Kenyatta an editor for [*Facing Mount Kenya*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0020.md).[^5]
