[George Padmore](Atlas/People/PER-0005.md) was a founder of the [International African Service Bureau](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0009.md) (IASB). From about June 1936 he had tried to launch a broader Pan-African Federation. Høgsbjerg dates the Bureau's launch to May 1937, with Padmore as chair and [C. L. R. James](Atlas/People/PER-0014.md) as editorial director.[^1] Reddock also names him among its founders.[^2] Special Branch later judged that the Bureau's policy would "remain in the hands of the Trotskyist, George Padmore".[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Founding date.** Høgsbjerg dates the launch to May 1937; Fryer writes that the Bureau replaced the IAFA in March 1937.[^1][^3]
