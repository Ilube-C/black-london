In 1789 [Olaudah Equiano](Atlas/People/PER-0006.md) published his autobiography, [*The Interesting Narrative*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0003.md), himself. The title page of the first volume states that it was "Printed for and sold by the author, no. 10, Union-Street, Middlesex Hospital".[^1] His dedication to the Lords and Commons is dated "Union-Street, Mary-le-bone, March 24, 1789".[^2] The address is therefore where the book could be bought from its author in 1789 (see [No. 10 Union Street](Atlas/Places/PLC-0016.md)).

## Evidence notes

- **Residence.** The title page and dedication give the address for sales and correspondence. The sources consulted do not say how long Equiano lived there.
