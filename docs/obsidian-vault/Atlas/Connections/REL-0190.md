*Claudia Jones and Paul Robeson were friends and colleagues; in London her West Indian Gazette held receptions for Robeson in 1959 and featured him at its anniversary concert in 1960.*

[Claudia Jones](Atlas/People/PER-0018.md) and [Paul Robeson](Atlas/People/PER-0019.md) knew each other in the United States. Sherwood records that Robeson joined a delegation that asked the Parole Board for her early release in May 1955.[^1] On the day of her farewell reception in New York in December 1955, he "had visited Claudia that afternoon" and sent a message saying "Claudia belongs to us".[^2] In London, Sherwood counts the Robesons among Jones's "earliest friendships in Britain".[^3] Her [*West Indian Gazette*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0022.md) gave a "Meet Paul Robeson" reception at Lambeth Town Hall on 27 June 1959 and another at [St Pancras Town Hall](Atlas/Places/PLC-0059.md) on 18 July.[^4] The next year Robeson was featured at the *Gazette*'s anniversary concert.[^5]

## Evidence notes

- **The extent of the friendship.** Sherwood writes that without access to the Robesons' papers "it is not possible to give an account of this undoubtedly close relationship".[^4]
