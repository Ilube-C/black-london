*Stuart Hall edited Universities and Left Review at its Soho offices above the Partisan Coffee House, 7 Carlisle Street, in the late 1950s.*

In the late 1950s [Stuart Hall](Atlas/People/PER-0022.md) taught at a school in Stockwell in the mornings. In the afternoons, he writes, "I would head up to Soho to the ULR office to edit the journal".[^1] The Bishopsgate Institute records that Raphael Samuel, Hall and Eric Hobsbawm founded the [Partisan Coffee House](Atlas/Places/PLC-0070.md) at 7 Carlisle Street in 1958, with "a library and offices of the ULR" upstairs.[^2] Hall's memoir describes the renovated offices in Carlisle Street, with a clubhouse, a library and a coffee bar.[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Whose café.** Bishopsgate names Hall among the café's founders. In his memoir Hall credits it to Samuel, who went ahead after an editorial meeting voted against it.[^2][^1]
