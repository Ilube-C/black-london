[Olive Morris](Atlas/People/PER-0025.md) and Liz Turnbull, later Liz Obi, occupied a flat above a launderette at [121 Railton Road](Atlas/Places/PLC-0081.md), Brixton, and fought off attempts at illegal eviction.[^1] A photograph of Morris climbing onto the roof later became the cover of the 1979 *Squatters Handbook*.[^1] The Lambeth Archives catalogue records that they "opened the 121 Railton Road squat" and resisted three illegal evictions.[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **Date.** Libcom gives 1972 for the occupation; the Lambeth Archives catalogue gives 1973.[^1][^2]
