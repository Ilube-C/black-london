[Paul Robeson](Atlas/People/PER-0019.md) played the title role in the [Stage Society's production](Atlas/Events/EVT-0018.md) of [C. L. R. James](Atlas/People/PER-0014.md)'s [*Toussaint Louverture*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0014.md) at the [Westminster Theatre](Atlas/Places/PLC-0049.md) on 15 and 16 March 1936.[^1] Duberman writes that Robeson accepted an offer "to portray Toussaint L'Ouverture in a stage play by the radical Trinidadian C. L. R. James".[^2] Adi and Sherwood see the role as part of his search "for more acceptable acting roles".[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Performances.** Høgsbjerg gives the dates as 15 and 16 March 1936. Duberman writes that the play "was given on several Sunday evenings in March 1936".[^1][^2]
