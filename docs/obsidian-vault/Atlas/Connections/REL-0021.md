[Amy Ashwood Garvey](Atlas/People/PER-0004.md) was among the founders of the [International African Service Bureau](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0009.md) (IASB) in 1937.[^1] Høgsbjerg records her as its vice-president, with [George Padmore](Atlas/People/PER-0005.md) as chair and [C. L. R. James](Atlas/People/PER-0014.md) as editorial director.[^2][^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Founding date.** Høgsbjerg dates the launch to May 1937; Fryer writes that the Bureau replaced the IAFA in March 1937.[^3][^4]
