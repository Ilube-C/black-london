[Harold Moody](Atlas/People/PER-0012.md) founded the [League of Coloured Peoples](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0019.md). Fryer writes that it was born "at a meeting in the [Central YMCA](Atlas/Places/PLC-0040.md), Tottenham Court Road, London, on 13 March 1931" (see [the founding meeting](Atlas/Events/EVT-0017.md)).[^1] Adi and Sherwood write that in 1931 Moody "decided to set up his own organisation".[^2] He was its president until his death in 1947. A contemporary said that "the League was his life and … he was the life of the League".[^1] English Heritage notes that he founded it while living at [164 Queen's Road](Atlas/Places/PLC-0039.md).[^3]
