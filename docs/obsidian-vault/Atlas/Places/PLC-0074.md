Rivington Place, in Shoreditch, was commissioned by two arts organisations, Iniva (the Institute of International Visual Arts) and Autograph ABP (the Association of Black Photographers). [Stuart Hall](Atlas/People/PER-0022.md) chaired both (see [Iniva & Autograph ABP](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0029.md)).[^1] Martin Jacques's biography of Hall calls him the central figure in its creation, and records that the building opened in 2007.[^1]
