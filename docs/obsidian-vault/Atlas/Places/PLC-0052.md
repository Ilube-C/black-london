*Whitelands College in Putney, the teacher-training college where Constance Cummings-John trained in the mid-1930s.*

Whitelands College, in Putney, is the site of the college described under [Whitelands College](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0021.md). Sierra Leone Web records that [Constance Cummings-John](Atlas/People/PER-0015.md) went to England at seventeen "and entered Whitelands College in Putney, where she trained as a teacher".[^1] Adi and Sherwood write that she was sent to London in 1935 and qualified in a year. While in London she took part in the [West African Students' Union](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0018.md) and the [League of Coloured Peoples](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0019.md).[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **Location.** The sources consulted name Putney but give no street address. The college's site in the 1930s is not described.
