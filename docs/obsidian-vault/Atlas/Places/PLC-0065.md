When [Paul Robeson](Atlas/People/PER-0019.md) enrolled at the [School of Oriental Studies](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0026.md) in 1934 to study Swahili and phonetics, the school was housed at Finsbury Circus in the City of London.[^1][^2] SOAS's archivists write that it had occupied the former London Institution building there since 1917. They add that it later moved temporarily to Vandon House in Victoria before settling in Bloomsbury.[^2] The school still holds Robeson's admission form.[^1] [Jomo Kenyatta](Atlas/People/PER-0017.md) also worked for the school as a linguistic informant in the 1930s.[^3] The sources consulted do not say where that work took place.
