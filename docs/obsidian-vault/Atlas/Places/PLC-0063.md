*The Savoy Theatre in the Strand, where Paul Robeson played Othello in 1930.*

In 1930 [Paul Robeson](Atlas/People/PER-0019.md) gave his first performance as Othello, at the Savoy Theatre (see [Othello, 1930](Atlas/Events/EVT-0020.md)).[^1][^2] English Heritage notes that the production "won many plaudits".[^3] Morrison writes that Robeson "was the first actor of African descent to appear in an extended-run Shakespearean production at a leading West End venue". Ira Aldridge had last appeared in London sixty-five years earlier.[^4] Peggy Ashcroft, then twenty-two, played Desdemona, and Morrison describes the "twenty curtain calls" on the first night.[^4] Duberman describes the rehearsals from early April 1930 and the opening night.[^5]
