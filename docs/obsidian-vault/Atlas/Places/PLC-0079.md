*Shakespeare Road, Brixton, where the British Black Panther Movement had its centre from 1969, with a Black People's Information Centre at No. 38.*

After Obi Egbuna's withdrawal at the end of 1968, Wild writes, the [Black Panther Movement](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0031.md)'s centre moved from Portobello Road in Notting Hill to Shakespeare Road in Brixton.[^1] The movement ran a Black People's Information Centre at 38 Shakespeare Road. Plays, poetry readings and concerts were held there.[^2] Liz Obi describes the Panthers' youth collective, which [Olive Morris](Atlas/People/PER-0025.md) joined, as based at 38 Shakespeare Road.[^3] Shakespeare Road later housed the offices of *Race Today*, at No. 74, before they moved to [165 Railton Road](Atlas/Places/PLC-0051.md) in 1982.[^4]
