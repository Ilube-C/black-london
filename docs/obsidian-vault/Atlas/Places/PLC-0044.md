[Una Marson](Atlas/People/PER-0013.md)'s play [*At What a Price*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0011.md), first staged in Kingston in 1932, was performed in London by the [League of Coloured Peoples](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0019.md) on 23 November 1933 at the YWCA's Central Club in Great Russell Street.[^1] It then transferred to the Scala Theatre in Charlotte Street for a three-night run from 15 January 1934.[^2] Marson directed it. Her biographer, Delia Jarrett-Macauley, calls it the first Black colonial production in the West End.[^2]

## Evidence notes

- **Date.** An earlier version of this atlas dated the Scala run to January 1933. Jarrett-Macauley's account places it in January 1934, after the November 1933 performance.[^2]
- **The building today.** No official record of the theatre's present status was consulted for this edition.
