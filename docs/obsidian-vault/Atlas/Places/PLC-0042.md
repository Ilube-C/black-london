*The house at 29 Brunswick Square, Camberwell, to which Una Marson moved from the Moodys' home in spring 1935.*

[Una Marson](Atlas/People/PER-0013.md) lodged with Harold Moody's family at [164 Queens Road](Atlas/Places/PLC-0039.md), Peckham, from her arrival in 1932. Jarrett-Macauley records that in spring 1935 she moved out, to 29 Brunswick Square, a road in Camberwell.[^1] In October that year she began working for the [Abyssinian Legation](Atlas/Places/PLC-0045.md).[^1]

## Evidence notes

- **Location.** An earlier version of this atlas placed the address in Bloomsbury. Jarrett-Macauley places it in Camberwell, and this edition follows her.[^1]
- **Length of stay.** The sources consulted do not say how long Marson lived at the house.
