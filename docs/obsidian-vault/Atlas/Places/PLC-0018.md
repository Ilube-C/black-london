[Olaudah Equiano](Atlas/People/PER-0006.md) died in London on 31 March 1797.[^1] Carretta records the burial entry of 6 April 1797 for the chapel's burying ground in Tottenham Court Road, the burial ground of Whitefield's Tabernacle.[^2] It is the last London place associated with Equiano in the sources consulted. The chapel itself appears in the twentieth century in a different context. [Una Marson](Atlas/People/PER-0013.md)'s biographer describes a special service at "Whitefields Tabernacle on Tottenham Court Road" in the early 1930s, at which the minister addressed his largely Black congregation as "brethren".[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **The grave.** The sources consulted do not record the position of Equiano's grave or whether it survives.
