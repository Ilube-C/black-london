When [Samuel Coleridge-Taylor](Atlas/People/PER-0010.md) entered the [Royal College of Music](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0015.md) in 1890, the College, Sayers writes, "was then housed in the old buildings which are now the home of the Royal College of Organists".[^1] There he was interviewed by the Director, Sir George Grove. In 1892 Grove arranged for him to study composition with Charles Villiers Stanford.[^1][^2] He studied at the College from the Christmas Term of 1890 to the Easter Term of 1897.[^3] On 11 November 1898 a College concert conducted by Stanford gave the first performance of [*Hiawatha's Wedding Feast*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0007.md) (see [the premiere](Atlas/Events/EVT-0015.md)).[^4]

## Evidence notes

- **Which building.** This record follows Sayers in identifying the College's building in 1890. He describes the venue of the 1898 premiere as "the old hall of the Royal College".[^4]
