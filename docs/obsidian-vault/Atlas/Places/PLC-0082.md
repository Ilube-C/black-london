*St Thomas' Hospital, Lambeth, where Olive Morris died on 12 July 1979.*

[Olive Morris](Atlas/People/PER-0025.md) died at St Thomas' Hospital, London, on 12 July 1979.[^1] Liz Obi records that she had come to Britain from Jamaica as a child, and that she was buried in Streatham Vale Cemetery.[^1] Her death came only weeks before the first issue of *FOWAAD*, the newsletter of the [Organisation of Women of African and Asian Descent](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0033.md) that she had helped to found. That issue carried a tribute, "In Memory of Olive Morris".[^2] The [Brixton Black Women's Group](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0032.md) later organised memorial services and evenings of remembrance for her.[^3]
