English Heritage records that "At the peak of his fame in 1929–30" [Paul Robeson](Atlas/People/PER-0019.md) lived at The Chestnuts, 1 Branch Hill, in Hampstead.[^1] These were the months between his success in *Show Boat* at [Drury Lane](Atlas/Places/PLC-0062.md) in 1928 and his first *Othello* at the [Savoy Theatre](Atlas/Places/PLC-0063.md) in 1930.[^2] Adi and Sherwood note that Robeson and his family made their home in London in the late 1920s and 1930s.[^2] English Heritage marked the house with a blue plaque in 2002. It reads: "PAUL ROBESON 1898-1976 Singer and Actor lived here 1929-1930".[^1] The sources consulted do not describe his daily life at the house.
