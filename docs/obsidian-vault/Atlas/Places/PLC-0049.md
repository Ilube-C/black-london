*The Westminster Theatre, where the Stage Society staged C. L. R. James's play Toussaint Louverture, with Paul Robeson in the title role, on 15 and 16 March 1936.*

On 15 and 16 March 1936 the Stage Society staged [C. L. R. James](Atlas/People/PER-0014.md)'s play [*Toussaint Louverture*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0014.md) at the Westminster Theatre, with [Paul Robeson](Atlas/People/PER-0019.md) in the title role (see [the production](Atlas/Events/EVT-0018.md)).[^1] Høgsbjerg describes the Stage Society as "prestigious".[^1] The play told the story of the Haitian Revolution, which James went on to write about in [*The Black Jacobins*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0015.md) (1938).[^2] Adi and Sherwood note that Robeson led the cast.[^3]
