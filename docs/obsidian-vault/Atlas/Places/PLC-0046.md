Broadcasting House in Portland Place was the headquarters of the [British Broadcasting Corporation](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0020.md) when [Una Marson](Atlas/People/PER-0013.md) joined its Empire Service as a programme assistant on 3 March 1941.[^1] Her biographer is explicit that "Una did not work there". She had "a little, cramped office in the old Bedford College in Regents Park", then the temporary home of the Empire Talks Department.[^2] Her earlier freelance television work, from 1939, was at Alexandra Palace.[^3]

## Evidence notes

- **Correction.** The first edition of this atlas placed Marson's work at Broadcasting House. Jarrett-Macauley states that she did not work there. The record is kept so that the correction is visible, and her BBC work is described under [the BBC](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0020.md).[^2]
