[Beryl Gilroy](Atlas/People/PER-0021.md) left school teaching in 1982. The *Guardian* obituary by Peter Fraser with Jessica and Eric Huntley records that she then joined the University of London Institute of Education and the Inner London Education Authority's Centre for Multicultural Education. There she applied her knowledge of psychology to her teaching experience.[^1] She later took a doctorate in counselling psychology.[^1] Margaret Busby, writing in 1992, describes her as a child psychotherapist and multicultural researcher at the Institute.[^2] Her first novel, [*Frangipani House*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0024.md), appeared in 1986.[^1]
