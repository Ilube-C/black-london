*Amy Ashwood Garvey's home at 1 Bassett Road, Ladbroke Grove, where a meeting on the 1958 riots was held and where a plaque to her was unveiled in 2009.*

1 Bassett Road, in Ladbroke Grove, was the home of [Amy Ashwood Garvey](Atlas/People/PER-0004.md) in the late 1950s. After the Notting Hill riots of 1958 she founded the Association for the Advancement of Coloured People. [Claudia Jones](Atlas/People/PER-0018.md) was its general secretary, and in September 1958 it held a meeting about the riots at Ashwood Garvey's home at 1 Bassett Road.[^1] The Jamaica Information Service reported that a plaque honouring Ashwood Garvey was unveiled "on November 5" 2009 "at 1 Bassett Road, in Ladbroke Grove, West London".[^2]
