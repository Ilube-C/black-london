*The house in Muswell Hill, north London, bought by Adelaide Tambo, which was the family home of Oliver Tambo during his years of exile.*

[Oliver Tambo](Atlas/People/PER-0020.md)'s family reached London in September 1960.[^1] Callinicos writes that Adelaide Tambo later bought a house in Muswell Hill. She raised the deposit after some fifteen years of working double shifts in hospitals, with help from anti-apartheid organisations.[^2] It was the house in which Tambo lived when he was in London. Callinicos records visitors there and Tambo's presence there in 1985.[^3] Some dissidents within the movement criticised the house.[^4] The family flew home to South Africa in December 1990.[^5]
