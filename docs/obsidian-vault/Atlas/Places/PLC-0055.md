*The London School of Economics, where Jomo Kenyatta studied social anthropology under Bronisław Malinowski from 1935 and Kwame Nkrumah attended lectures in 1945.*

The London School of Economics is the site of the school described under [London School of Economics](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0023.md). [Jomo Kenyatta](Atlas/People/PER-0017.md) was Malinowski's personal student by the spring of 1935. That autumn he enrolled for the three-year postgraduate diploma in social anthropology, and he submitted his thesis at the end of 1937.[^1] It became [*Facing Mount Kenya*](Atlas/Works/WRK-0020.md) (1938). In his introduction Malinowski wrote that Kenyatta had been "a member of my discussion class at the London School of Economics" for several years.[^2] [Kwame Nkrumah](Atlas/People/PER-0016.md) attended lectures there in 1945 and met Harold Laski, who "lectured there on political science".[^3]
