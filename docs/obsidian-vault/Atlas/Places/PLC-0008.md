*The college in central London where Henry Sylvester Williams studied from 1896 and, according to Fryer, Harold Moody studied medicine from 1904.*

King's College London is the site of the institution described under [King's College London](Atlas/Organisations/ORG-0005.md). [Henry Sylvester Williams](Atlas/People/PER-0003.md) arrived in London in 1896 and "enrolled at King's College as an evening student taking Latin", according to Adi and Sherwood.[^1] Clarke writes that he came to study law there.[^2] Fryer writes that [Harold Moody](Atlas/People/PER-0012.md) came from Jamaica in 1904 to study medicine at King's College, qualified in 1910, and was then refused a hospital house appointment.[^3] Adi and Sherwood describe Moody more generally as a medical student at London University from 1904.[^4]

## Evidence notes

- **The building.** The sources consulted do not say which of the college's buildings either man used.
