# Black London — knowledge graph

This is the **knowledge graph** for [Black London](https://black-london.vercel.app/), a curated map of Black History sites, events and restaurants across the city. Every site, person, theme, era, borough, walk, event and restaurant in the dataset is a note here, with `[[wikilinks]]` between anything that connects.

It exists to make the *shape* of the dataset visible — easier to see which themes are well covered, which people are isolated, which sites cluster — and to give curators a quick way to navigate connections without scrolling a list.

## See the graph

Click the **graph icon** in the top-right of any page (the small circular-network icon). Zoom out for the whole lattice; click any node to focus on its neighbours and read its note.
