# Dr Harold Moody — Queen's Road, Peckham
> Home and surgery of Harold Moody, founder of the League of Coloured Peoples (1931) — the leading Black civil-rights organisation in pre-war Britain.
Harold Moody, born in Kingston, Jamaica in 1882, qualified as a doctor at King's College Hospital after a struggle against the colour bar in medicine. He set up his surgery at 164 Queen's Road, Peckham — and from his front room founded the League of Coloured Peoples on 13 March 1931.

The League campaigned against the racial bar in employment, housing and the merchant navy; lobbied the Colonial Office over the case of Learie Constantine; and laid the political groundwork that the Windrush generation would build upon. Moody died in 1947, weeks after returning from a fundraising tour of the Caribbean.
